One tap does both: hear and select. Your instructions say »Tapni stavek, nato obraz«, so I dropped the separate 🔍 button from turn 1. Tapping a sentence plays it and picks it up. The two states are shown in different ways:
· heard stays forever as a dotted underline plus 👂, like ink
· selected is a solid hue fill with white text and 👆 or 👇 pointing to where the next tap goes, and it lasts until the next tap.
The helper is the child’s chosen pomočnik (🦉🐶🐱🦊 from SIDEKICKS) and sits in one bubble at the bottom. Hints follow your ladder: specific → case h1 → »svetli stavki« glow yellow → »utripa« pulses the answer. The bubble never auto-plays. It speaks only when tapped.
Every Slovenian string is from slovenian-text.json. The only exception is the step title for the call, where I used »Zvoni!« from screen_strings.
Stamps: »Primer rešen!« is red, »NAPAČEN ČAS!« is blue and »To šteje!« is green. Red appears only on positive stamps, never on mistakes.
Turn 1 (system sheet, placeholder copy) is still in Detektiv.dc.html. Its components still apply. Only the 🔍 button is gone.
