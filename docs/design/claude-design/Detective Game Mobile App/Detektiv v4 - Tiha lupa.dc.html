A quiet notebook, with the season as a window at the top
From 2c it keeps the light, calm paper. From 3b it keeps the season world (now one deep band at the top that holds the question), the glow for »svetel«, the ⚽ on the step line, the cases as a path, and the small tap animations. The changes: thin outlines instead of thick sticker edges, soft shadows instead of hard ones, two font weights, and fewer things on each screen.
Emoji always sit on a white coin. This fixes the 👆 that disappeared on yellow. Any emoji placed on a coloured surface gets a white circle under it, so it stays visible in every season colour.
Gold now means only light. Picked is the deep season colour with white text. Gold is used only for the hint glow and highlighted words, so the two can never be confused.
Calm motion. Movements are 4–6 px, nothing bounces forever, and the ball slides to the next stop. The only loops are the helper breathing (4 s) and a slow hint glow (2 s). Exciting moments, like the stamp and the newspaper, happen once and end.
Type. Lexend 400 for reading and 600 for titles, with Verdana as fallback, and full č š ž. Sentences are 21–22 px, labels at least 15 px.
