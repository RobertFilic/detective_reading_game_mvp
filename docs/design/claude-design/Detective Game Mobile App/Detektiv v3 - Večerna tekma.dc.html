3Redesign: »Večerna tekma«, a detective kit under the floodlights
3aThe idea, and the system
The story happens in the dark. The clues are in the light.
Each season is a world at dusk: a floodlit pitch for ⚽ pokal, the night sea for 🦔 galeb, snow under the moon for ❄️ sneg. Anything the child reads or taps is a bright paper sticker sitting in that light. Your copy already says »svetel stavek« and »svetle besede«, so the hint is literally light: hinted sentences glow and nothing else gets dimmed. Spooky but cosy: you’re out late, the lamps are warm, and nothing bad is there.

Type: Lexend, with Verdana as fallback. Lexend was designed to help people read more fluently. It’s free and includes č š ž ć đ. Verdana is on almost every device. Sentences are 22–24 px at weight 400, labels 500, titles 800. Nothing in the app is smaller than 15 px.
Three colours, three meanings. Gold means picked or go, blue means heard, red means a stamp. The season changes only the world hue and its emoji (⚽ 🦔 ❄️). An adult can make a new season by changing one number.
Sticker shapes. Every target has a 3 px ink outline and a hard 4 px shadow, so it looks like something you can press. That separates targets from text that isn’t tappable. Targets are 56 px minimum, and 64–76 px for anything the child reads.
The ball is the progress. In every case, a row of stops runs across the top, and the season emoji (⚽) hops to the step you’re on. The season board works the same way: the cases form a path joined by a string.

The mechanics, copy and hint ladder are the same as 2c. What changed:
· Board: the cases are a path joined by a string, so the order (»Zgodba gre po vrsti«) can be seen. The mystery is the gold card at the top.
· Scene: objects you haven’t searched sit in the dark. Each one lights up and becomes paper when you find it.
· Faces: sticker-album cards, crossed off with a pencil ✕.
· Hints: »svetel« sentences really glow.
· Progress: the ⚽ hops along the stops at the top.

In Tweaks, season switches the world (pitch, sea or snow) by changing one hue.
