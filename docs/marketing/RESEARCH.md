# Family research: interviews and the holiday diary

The family researcher's guide for the December validation (VALIDATION.md). The numbers say *what* families do; this says *why*. Draft 2026-11-19. Questions are Slovenian drafts for the Slovenian editor. Keep this file under ~130 lines; findings go to `docs/marketing/FINDINGS.md`, one dated entry per family, as in PLAYTEST.md.

## Rules
- **The producer talks to the families.** The researcher writes the guides, reads the notes and finds the patterns.
- **Ask about the past, not the future.** »Kaj ste počeli včeraj zvečer?« beats »Bi uporabljali …?«. People are kind about ideas and honest about what they did.
- **Don't sell.** Show the game only at the end of the first interview, or not at all.
- **No names in the notes:** »družina 3, deklica 7, mama«. The child is never interviewed alone; the parent is present and agrees out loud.
- **Write what you saw and heard,** word for word where it matters.

## Who to talk to (screener)
15–30 families, mixed on purpose:
- A child of 7–8 (6 and 9 are fine as extras), learning to read in Slovenian.
- **Both kinds of parent:** the very engaged one who limits screens, and the relaxed one who lets the child use the tablet. Ask: »Koliko časa na dan je vaš otrok pred zaslonom, ob delavnikih?« and »Imate doma pravila za zaslone?«
- Not only friends: ask friends to pass the invitation on, so at least a third are families you don't know well.
- Ask, too: who reads with the child at home, and does the child have their own device?

## Interview 1: before (20 minutes, in person or a call)
**The routine.**
1. »Opišite mi včerajšnji dan od prihoda iz šole do spanja.«
2. »Kdaj otrok bere? Sam ali z vami? Kako je bilo zadnjič?«
3. »Kaj je pri branju najtežje: da začne, da razume, ali da vztraja?«

**Screens** (H3).
4. »Kdaj otrok sme na tablico ali telefon? Kdo odloči?«
5. »Kaj vas pri zaslonih skrbi? Se je kdaj kaj zgodilo, da ste rekli: dovolj?«
6. »Ali je kakšna aplikacija ali igra, ki se vam zdi v redu? Zakaj prav ta?«

**Playing together** (H1 vs H2).
7. »Kdaj ste se nazadnje igrali kaj skupaj? Kaj je bilo?«
8. »Če bi otrok igral bralno igro, bi raje sedeli zraven ali bi bili kje drugje? Zakaj?«
9. »Koliko minut na dan bi realno imeli za tak skupni čas?«

**Only at the end:** show the leaflet (both variants, side by side): »Kateri bi vzeli s police? Zakaj?« Note the first words they say.

## The holiday diary: »Detektivske počitnice« (12-22 → 01-04)
- **The deal:** play as you like (no minimum), and after each sitting answer one card. At the end, the child gets the diploma on 12-31 (the game) and the family a thank-you.
- **The card** (a short form or a message to the producer, 1 minute, after each sitting):
  1. Kdo je igral? (otrok sam / skupaj / otrok, odrasel je bil v bližini)
  2. Koliko časa, približno?
  3. Kdo je predlagal, da igrate? (otrok / odrasel)
  4. En stavek: kaj je bilo najboljše ali najtežje?
  5. (če ste igrali skupaj) Kaj ste počeli vi: brali, pomagali, samo gledali?
- **A nudge mid-way** (12-29): »Kako gre? Je otrok kaj omenil zgodbo, ko niste igrali?« (H4: the story away from the screen).
- The diary sits beside the game's anonymous events; they are **never joined** (no family is matched to its rows). The diary is the family's own report.

## Interview 2: after (15 minutes, 01-05 → 01-11)
1. »Kako je izgledalo, ko ste igrali? Opišite zadnjič.«
2. »Kdo je največkrat predlagal igro?« (the pull, H4)
3. »Se vam je zdelo kot čas pred zaslonom ali kot branje? Zakaj?« (H3)
4. »Ste bili zraven? Bi bili raje bolj ali manj?« (H1/H2)
5. »Kaj bi spremenili, da bi igrali pogosteje?«
6. »Bi jo priporočili? Komu, in kaj bi mu rekli?« (the words they'd use: copy for the landing page)
7. »Bi za nove primere kaj plačali? Koliko se vam zdi pošteno?« (a note for later, not a decision now)

## Teachers (H5, side test, 3 conversations at most)
- »Kako učencem priporočate branje za doma?« · »Bi priporočili tudi igro za telefon? Kaj bi morala imeti?« · »Bi letak dali staršem na govorilnih urah?«

## The synthesis (researcher, after each 5 families and at the end)
≤ 40 lines in FINDINGS.md:
- **For each hypothesis H1–H5:** for / against / unclear, with the families' own words (short quotes, family number).
- **The words parents use** for the problem and the value (straight to the copywriter).
- **Surprises:** what nobody predicted.
- **What it means** for the product (to the editorial director) and for the messages (to the copywriter).
