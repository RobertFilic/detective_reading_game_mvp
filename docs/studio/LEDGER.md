## Worn out: avoid or vary next season

- **»X ima (zelo) rad(a) Y« as the decoy**: used in 8 of 18 cases. A child can learn »likes it = decoy« without reading the rest. Next season: decoys are things that *happened* (was near, was seen, has a tool), and at least once »likes it« is the real clue.
- **»vso noč« in alibis**: in most Bip alibis. Vary: a witness, an object (a ticket, a photo), being too small or too big, being busy elsewhere with someone, a lock they can't open.
- **Tim as culprit**: 4 of 12 space cases. Rest him for a season.
- **Food as motive**: about a third of the cases. Use other wishes: to help, to surprise, to fix, to see, to win, to hide a mistake.
- **The proof always names the culprit plainly.** Next skill rungs need proofs that point through a trace, a time or a missing thing.
- **Every question starts »Kdo je …?«.** Fine for now; a later season can ask »Kaj se je zgodilo?« or »Zakaj …?«.
- **The culprit as the first face.** The faces show in `suspects` order (no shuffle); in 🦔 g1–g4 the culprit was the leftmost face four cases running. Keep one cast order for the season (as Bip did) or spread the culprit's place.
- **The lie as the top claim bubble.** Claims show in `lie.claims` order; in 🦔 g3–g6 (every case with two claims) the lie was first. Vary it.
- **The proof under the object the question names** (👾 b4 antenna, 🦔 g3 bell, g5 castle, g1 castle from the call). Once a season, hide it somewhere else.
- **The proof as the culprit's possessive** (»Lanova nalepka«, »Zojin copat«, »Ivovi mizi«, »Lanovi torbi«, »Zojinega kostuma«): 5 of 6 🦕 cases, while alibis never use one. Once the decoy is stamped NAPAČNA SLED, »the other sentence with a suspect's -ov/-in« is the proof. Vary: a proof that names nobody (a pair), a time, a missing thing. ⚽ p1–p3 again: »Zojinih rokavicah«, »Borovem rogu«, »Hanine knjige«, each the case's only culprit possessive.
- **The proof in the bottom two rows** of the solve list (rows follow `sentences` order): 🦕 m2, m3, m4, m6. Put it in the upper half sometimes. ⚽ overcorrected: row 3 or 4 of 9 in p1–p4. Spread it (top, middle, bottom).
