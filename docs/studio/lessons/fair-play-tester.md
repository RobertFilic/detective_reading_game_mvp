# Lessons: fair-play tester

Read before every task. Add up to 3 lessons after every task: the rule, then — evidence (what happened, where). A lesson confirmed twice moves up to **Rules**; a lesson proved wrong is deleted. Keep this file under ~60 lines. Playtest evidence beats review opinion.

## Rules
- **Write the deed's time window and place, then test every alibi against it**, as the child sees it on the solve screen (an ask step's question is not shown). No time = no alibi; a time with no length (»ponoči« vs »vso noč«/»do jutra«) is weak. — playtest Galeb 1 (2026-11-02), then audit 1 found it in v2, v3, v5, b2–b5, g1–g5 (2026-11-05); muzej review: m4 question without »ob šestih«, m6 »ponoči« vs »ob polnoči«.

## Lessons
- Season-wide pattern found 2026-11-01: »ima rad« decoys in 8 of 18 cases, »vso noč« in most Bip alibis, Tim culprit in 4 of 12 space cases. Check the ledger's "Worn out" list for every new case. — LEDGER.md.
- A suspect's own claim (»Vso noč sem bil na polnilcu.«) is not an alibi; the game says »To trdi o sebi. Ali je res?«. Check that a claim is never marked `clear`. — Bip 2.
- When a nameless suspect (👀 »???«) is in the line-up, the answer can be guessed by elimination alone; make sure the child still has to read every alibi and the proof to finish. — Bip 6.
- The practice case's pointing finger landed on one animal, which gave the answer away; anything that points at one choice is a clue, check hints and animations too, not only sentences. — tutorial, 2026-10.
- Check the *order* things are shown in, not only what they say. The engine doesn't shuffle: faces follow `suspects`, claim bubbles follow `lie.claims`. The culprit was the leftmost face in 🦔 g1–g4 and the lie the top bubble in g3–g6. — Galeb review, 2026-11-01.
- In UGOVOR, try every row against every claim the way a 7-year-old reads time and opinion. »Gusti sedi na dimniku« (now) seems to break »zjutraj pri svetilniku«, and »torej je zvonil Bepi« breaks »sem še spal«, and the engine answers »Oba sta lahko res«. Each needs a `hmm` or a rewrite. Also check that a witness was awake (Luka »smrčal« yet saw Gusti). — Galeb g2, g3, g4.
- For every corkboard card, check that its »what it means at the end« is on screen before the guess. Vera's blue scarf showed up only in the final letter, so card 4 pointed only at Bepi. Letters arrive next session, so don't count a P. S. as a clue for a guess asked right after that case. — Galeb card 4, g5 letter.
- **Playtest miss (my PASS was wrong):** an alibi that doesn't say *when* is no alibi. »Vera je z balkona videla Bepija na morju« (no time, no length) next to the decoy »Bepi zna vezati vozle« left the child unable to choose between Luka and Bepi. Fixed with »Dopoldne …« in the call and »vse dopoldne« in the alibi. For every case, write the deed's time window and test each alibi against it. — Galeb 1, playtest 2026-11-02.
- In an ask step, the question (»Kdo je bil ponoči s teboj?«) is NOT on the solve screen; only »Name: answer« is. Test the answer text alone for time and place. »Luka je na barki smrčal« lost its »ponoči«, next to Luka's spade found »na hodniku« (also only in the question). — audit 2026-11-05: g2 s4/s2, g3 s1, b2 s1/s3/s6.
- A `clear` that is the suspect's own word is still a self-claim, even as the UGOVOR truth after a blush. It needs a backer that pins the same time (g3: »Ob zori sem se učil plavati« is backed only by »rokavčki so še mokri«, which has no time, so »woken by the bell, then swam« fits too). — audit 2026-11-05, g3.
- Read each decoy against the scene's facts, not alone: »Maja rada fotografira Luno« + »Na njej je nova slika Lune« makes the decoy the motive, and Maja's only alibi was her own word. Tim's Moon motive was only in the reveal. — audit 2026-11-05, v5.
- A fix that adds a sentence to explain a decoy can become a second alibi. g5 s11 »Zjutraj je Gusti pristala na podrtem gradu« (added to answer the feather) clears Gusti better than the geslo, but the engine only takes the geslo and says »Ta stavek ne pove, kdo ni bil«. After every fix, test each new sentence as a cross-off for every suspect it names. — audit 2, 2026-11-05.
- When a proof moves, the old proof stays a proof. b5 made the slipper the proof but kept »Za škatlami je skrito Timovo darilo« as neutral; Tim's gift in the storeroom still puts Tim there, and the engine answers »ne pove, kdo ni bil«. Grep every non-proof sentence that names the culprit. — audit 2, b5.
- Walk wrong taps through `cross()` in index.html, not only the data: proof → alibi → h1 on the 2nd wrong → liar messages → truthLead → selfClaim (only for non-clear) → notWho → notThis. A suspect's own »sva« alibi (b2 s1, g2 s4) gets notWho, and a neutral clue that names the culprit (g4 s1) gets notThis. — audit 2.
- A witness who gives someone's alibi must have been able to see it *at that hour*. m3: Mila puts Rado in the cellar »ob sedmih«, and Rado then says where Zoja was »ob sedmih«. In a case that teaches »a sure witness can be wrong«, that is the next thing a sharp child doubts. Check each witness's own place at the deed's hour. — muzej review, 2026-11-05.
- 🧭: test every row as an explanation of each clue, *and* each explanation as a cross-off against every face. An explanation that measures (m2 »Tačke so velike kot otroški čevelj«) is itself an alibi for the cat and for adult Rado, and the engine answers a right deduction with »ne pove, kdo ni bil« or a counted »Res, sled ne drži«. The culprit's proof (m2 s10, a cat slipper in paint) can explain the clue better than the chosen sentence. — muzej m2.
- A true 🧭 clue is the proof handed over before solve: »Vse sledi so preverjene« after the one wrong clue leaves the true arrow pointing straight at the culprit, and the proof alone solves (no cross-offs needed). Check the end state of every step for what it gives away, not only the taps. Also check the question carries the time: m4's question lost »ob šestih«, which was only in the call. — muzej m4.
