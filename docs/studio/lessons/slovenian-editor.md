# Lessons: Slovenian editor

Read before every task. Add up to 3 lessons after every task: the rule, then — evidence (what happened, where). A lesson confirmed twice moves up to **Rules**; a lesson proved wrong is deleted. Keep this file under ~60 lines. Playtest evidence beats review opinion.

## Rules
- All Slovenian in the game so far was drafted without a native speaker; treat every line as unchecked until the producer confirms a native read. — status 2026-11-01.

## Lessons
- Lines that address the child in the past tense need both genders (`ga(m, f)`), or a phrasing without gender: »Prav si ugibal / ugibala«. — Bip 6 guess result.
- Two singular subjects take the dual: »Žlička in žica sta skriti« / »sta bili«. — Bip 6.
- `caught` is read inside »Detektivka Ana je ujela ___!«: check the accusative there (Tima, Elo, Kiki, Bipa). — all Bip cases.
- The checker counts »…« as a word (`words()` doesn't strip it): »No ja … « already costs 3 of the 8. Count before adding a word. — Galeb g5 (»… pa je res bil« went to 9).
- Feminine names in -i (Rozi, Gusti): keep the noun undeclined (z Rozi, ujela Gusti, o Gusti). The producer (native speaker) chose the possessive **Rozin listek**, not Rozijin (2026-11-01, Galeb); use the short -in form for such names. — Galeb.
- The child addresses old ladies with **Vi** (»Kdaj greste spat?«, »Kaj ste videli?«): the producer's choice for gospa Vera (2026-11-01). Muzej: the writers mixed ti and Vi to the same adults (m2–m3 Vi, m6 ti); the editor chose **Vi for all museum staff** (Breda, Rambo, Ivo, Mila), **confirmed by the producer 2026-11-05**. Check each adult's address across the whole season, not per case; formal participle stays masculine plural even to a woman (»Kje ste bili?«). — Galeb, Muzej 2026-11-05.
- Swap colloquial forms for standard ones in child text (»ne paše« → »ni prava«, »s tabo« → »s teboj«), and check that a simile makes sense (»Hodnik škriplje kot plaža«: a beach doesn't creak). — Galeb g2, UI.
- UI headings and tags: prefer short words a child decodes at a glance over »pustolovščina« (13 letters, šč); reuse symbols the game already explains (🏅 = »Pustolovščina rešena!«). Standalone sticker »NOVO« needs no agreement (shop convention); unconfirmed by a native. — start screen labels, 2026-11-02.
- Instruction cards (»Najprej navodilo«): one action per sentence, goal question before the action (»Kaj je bilo najprej? Tapni …«), and point at an icon the child can see (»Tapni 💬«) instead of an abstract noun (»trditev«). Unconfirmed by a native. — how* texts, 2026-11-02.
- Impersonal sound verbs work (»Iz zračnika je piskalo«, »je ropotalo«), but not an action verb: »iz sobe 7 je tipkalo« sounds wrong, and »tipkalo« is also a noun (a push-button). Use a subject: »v sobi 7 je nekdo tipkal«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Galeb g6 s3, 2026-11-05.
- A time word with the present tense reads as a habit (»Opoldne so listi mokri« = every noon). Next to a past-tense line, keep the past: »Opoldne so bili listi …«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Vesolje v2 s2, 2026-11-05.
- Two names side by side read as one full name (»Tim Maji bral«); put the verb between them, neutral order verb → dative → object (»Tim bral Maji pravljico«, »učil Bepija plavati«). Unconfirmed by a native. — Bip b4 s1, Galeb g4 s7, 2026-11-05.
- Numbers 3 and 4 take the nominative plural: »Še trije / štirje dnevi«; 2 the dual »dva dneva«; 5+ the genitive »pet dni«. »Še štiri dni« is the common spoken slip. Confirmed by the producer (native speaker) 2026-11-05. — Muzej countdown.
- Scene `label`s are read alone under a picture: avoid a label that is also a function word (»kot« = corner, but reads as »like«); name the object (»zvezek« for 📓). — Muzej m1, 2026-11-05.
- Case data can't switch gender (only UI lines have `ga(m, f)`): »Hej, detektiv!« / »Hvala, detektiv!« in a call or letter speaks only to a boy. Use »Hej, pomagaj!«, »Hej!«, »Hvala!«. Same for two of anything: dual even without »dva« (»Rezultata je povedal Lan«, to match »rezultata obeh tekem«). Unconfirmed by a native. — Pokal p1, p2, p6, 2026-11-06.
- The checker does **not** count reveal / excuse / letter / card.think in step cases (`continue` at cases.mjs:312 skips line 324), and counts lock `h1`/`h2` as one whole line, not per sentence. Count those by hand (a 9-word reveal had passed: p2 »… skrivaj stekel k malemu golu«). — Pokal, 2026-11-06.
- A clitic can't open a sentence in standard Slovenian: »Ga odkriješ?« → »Ali ga odkriješ?«. A process noun can't be a plural's predicate: »Sive risbe so drgnjenje« → »… so odtisi«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Pokal cup q, p6 reveal, 2026-11-06.
- Parent-facing copy uses formal **vi** (»Povlecite«, »vi pomagate«), so a call to parent + child is **»Igrajte skupaj«**, not the dual »Igrajta« (the reader is one person). The install sheet still says ti (»Tapni«, »Izberi«): flagged, not changed. Unconfirmed by a native. — landing page, 2026-11-09.
- Onboarding knows the gender after the avatar pick (`S.mk.g`, not `profile()`, so `ga()` doesn't work there yet): use `m.g === 'f'` for lines after step 1 (»Postala si detektivka!«). — make() card helper, 2026-11-09.
- Watch calques from English design copy: »Veliki stavki« (= long, not big print) → »Velike črke«; logical »zaključek« → »sklep«; »brez nizov« (streaks) is opaque to parents. Unconfirmed by a native. — landing page, 2026-11-09.
- Foreign names follow the SP pattern: a final -y said as »i« takes j (City → Cityja, pri Cityju, like Kennedyja); a team takes the gender of its form (Barcelona je zmagala, City / Real / Bayern je zmagal). Check decoding separately: Slovenian letter-to-sound reading turns City into »ci-ti« and Kane into the verb »kane«; flag names whose spelling a 7-year-old reads wrong, even when the grammar is right. Unconfirmed by a native. — Pokal teams, 2026-11-12.
- Speech lines by a `who` count as one line for the 8 words (not per sentence): »Meee! Jaz sem maskota Reala. Imam bel kostum z rogovi!« failed at 10. Fold the facts into one sentence rather than drop one the clue needs (here white + horns). — Pokal p2 s2, 2026-11-12.
- Before »improving« a UI line, grep the game's own idiom: »Kdo ni bil?«, »Kdo NI mogel biti?«, »svetel stavek«, »kraj dogodka« are in every case and in how*/elim* lines, so new lines reuse them (»Kdo je bil? Tapni obraz.«, »Ostal je eden. Pokaži dokaz!«) instead of a new term. A warm-up is »vaja«, a real one »primer«. — warm-up, 2026-11-12.
- The companion and the child are two: dual, not plural (»Greva na pravi primer«, not »Gremo«). A bare noun as a question (»Zagata?«) is not idiomatic: »Ne gre?«. `odgovori` / `prosi` alone are ambiguous or stiff (imperative = 3rd person): »Vsak nekaj pove«, »kaj pravi«. Unconfirmed by a native. — lead*/goal* lines, 2026-11-12.
- Doubt about the rule »clitics never open a sentence«: a yes/no question without »ali« may start with one (»Je to res?« is in howLie, »Je ta sled prava?« left as is). Ask the producer; if confirmed, narrow the rule in sl.md to statements. — goalHerring, 2026-11-12.
- Templates that drop a noun into »Tu je ${name}« / »Kjer je …« break on plural-only nouns (očala, sani, vrata, hlače): »Tu je očala«. Mark them (`pl: true`) and switch je/so. Unconfirmed by a native. — Kje je kdo? očala, 2026-11-13.
- A word the rules define must not also be part of a place name: »Ob« = »levo, desno, zgoraj ali spodaj« in the rules, but »Iza je bila ob igrišču« is a room clue. Flag such room names (Ob igrišču → e.g. »Klop za igralce« / »Rob igrišča«). — Kje je kdo? u7, u12, 2026-11-13.
- Sport and household collocations: »dati koš« (not »vreči koš«), »ključ od knjižnice« (not »ključ knjižnice«), »kljuka« is a door handle, not a hook. Unconfirmed by a native. — Kje je kdo? u7, u9, 2026-11-13.
- »stati na« + locative (place), »usesti se na« + accusative (motion): »Na drevo ne more stati nihče« is wrong; safest is the game's own frame »Kjer je drevo, … ne more stati nihče«. — Kje je kdo? intro card 5, 2026-11-13.
- Side games reuse the main game's button idiom: dual »Začniva!« / »Igrajva!« (helper + child), as in solo warmGo; English UI nouns like »tap« (»Še en tap …«) become a verb clause: »Če polje tapneš še enkrat, …«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Kje je kdo? intro / notes, 2026-11-13.
- Gender suffix and name chosen by different conditions break: `bil${p.she?'a':''} ${free ? p.name : 'nekdo'}` gives »bila nekdo«. Check both branches of every template, not only the happy path. — Kje je kdo? noteBtn, 2026-11-13.
- Privacy copy: check the text against the code, not only the brief. »Zraven pošljemo številko uganke, …« read as a complete list, but `track()` in kje-je-kdo also sends hint/wrong/undo/notes counts, an `intro` event and a `who` id. Report gaps as findings; don't invent wording for them. — zasebnost, 2026-11-14.
- A product name as a bare genitive is ambiguous: »v ugankah Skrivnostne sobe« reads as »of the secret room«. Quote it and give it a head noun: »v igri »Skrivnostne sobe««. GDPR citations in Slovenian follow the official text: »točka (f) člena 6(1)«. Unconfirmed by a native. — zasebnost, 2026-11-14.
- Privacy copy, second time (confirms the 2026-11-14 lesson): when a new sender is added, re-read the **older** promises too. »Ti podatki naprave ne zapustijo« (section 2) became false once solo `start` sent `lvl` (the reading level the adult set); a brief that says »ki ju nastavi igra« was also wrong for it. Grep each sent key back to the list of on-device data. — zasebnost solo events, 2026-11-14.
- Adult rating labels must be gender-free and read as a result: »(sam, s pomočjo, težko)« → »(brez pomoči, s pomočjo ali s težavo)«. Unconfirmed by a native. — zasebnost verdict, 2026-11-14.
- Copied story lines carry their setting: »Doma so bili štirje« (from puzzle 1) landed in a shop puzzle → »Tam so bili štirje«. A question »v sobi« is ambiguous when the map also has »Dnevna soba«: name the room in full (»v otroški sobi«). — Skrivnostne sobe 13, 19, 2026-11-15.
- Verb pairs that look alike: »zavezati balon« = knot its neck, »privezati« = tie it to something; »zamuditi« = miss (the train), »zamujati« = be late (and still catch it). Purpose clause: »da bi vlak počakal«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Skrivnostne sobe 14, 20, 2026-11-15.
- »na« + a water room (»na ribniku«, »na jezeru«) reads as on the water. When the clue word can't change, flag the room name instead. Unconfirmed by a native. — Skrivnostne sobe 20, 2026-11-15.
- Hand-written intro copy outside the checker ran over 8 words in 5 of 7 lines (a name before the comma is 2 words; »💡 Če se zatakneš, ti pomoč pokaže naslednji korak« = 9). Count every line of new HTML by hand; split a rule and its example into two sentences. — Skrivnostne sobe intro cards, 2026-11-15.
- »postaviti koga na mesto« also means »put someone in their place« (scold): write »na pravo mesto«. Reuse the game's own rule lines in intro cards (»V vsaki vrsti je samo en človek«, button names »Zapiski«, »Pomoč«), and let the last card echo the first rule's question (»Kdo je bil sam?«) so the cards read as one chain. Unconfirmed by a native. — Skrivnostne sobe intro, 2026-11-15.
- Writers clip long nouns into another word: »v sad / v sadu« for the orchard (»sad« = fruit) → »v sadovnjak / v sadovnjaku«. Check every shortened noun against the place name. — Sneg n2, 2026-11-16.
- Fixed verb frames slip in drafts: »staviti se z kom« (not »stavila sem z Luko«), »umivati zobe« (everyday, over »čistiti«), »lovi snežinke z jezikom«, »je vsem mrzlo« (over personal »vsi zebejo«); an `ask` must keep the answer's frame: »meketa na Pio« → »Na koga Bor meketa?«, not »Komu«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Sneg n2, n3, n5, n6, 2026-11-16.
- A call line can't lean on a noun the call hasn't said yet: »BIM-BAM! / Včeraj ga je nekdo obesil« (ga = the bell, never named) → »… obesil zvonec«. Also »zvonec na vratih« reads as the doorbell, which rings from outside: name the season's own bell (Pijin zvonec). — Sneg n3, n6, 2026-11-16.
- Children read subject-first: »Listek drži bucika« (OVS, listek looks like the subject because nom = acc) → »Bucika drži listek«. Courtesy capital »Vas/Vam« belongs in letters, not in quoted speech: »Kdo je bil pri vas?« (as pokal »Kdo vam je pomagal«). Unconfirmed by a native. — Igrače i5 s4, ask s7, 2026-11-19.
- Aspect and collocation slips in drafts: one past event takes the perfective (»je ob šestih odnesel daljinec«, not »nesel«), a one-off tomorrow too (»Jutri Nuša napolni paketnik«); »zastavljati uganke« (not »postavljati«); »ko sem ga prinesla« over a bare »ko sem ga dala«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Igrače i4, i3, i6, 2026-11-19.
- A list line that answers a past-tense ask keeps the past (»Kaj si videla ob štirih?« → »Ob štirih je Gal brisal prah«), especially when another line of the same list is past (the torn line) and a habit line with the same verb sits nearby (»Gal briše skrinjice«). Second case of the »time word + present = habit« rule (Vesolje v2). — Igrače i2 s3, 2026-11-19.
- Rewrites bring back slips the first pass fixed: the countdown »Še štiri / tri dni« reappeared in new calls (native rule: »Še štirje / trije dnevi«). On a second pass, grep every rule from the first pass again, not only the lines that were flagged. — Igrače i2, i3 calls, 2026-11-19.
- An alibi written as a negation (»Zofka ni šla s trga«) can usually be said positively with the same fact (»je bila vse dopoldne na trgu«); do it when the season's negation knob is »rare«. »slikati punčke« reads as drawing or photographing them: for painting toys use »barvati«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Igrače i3 s5, s7, 2026-11-19.
- Look-alike verbs of motion: »zdrseti« = slip (an accident), »drsati« = skate, »drseti« = glide: »Zdrsela je kot labod« says she slipped → »Drsala je kot labod«. A pair of things takes the plural of their parts: »drsalke so imele topa rezila« (not »top rezilo«). Unconfirmed by a native. — Skrivnostne sobe 23, 2026-11-19.
- Possessives of names in -ia: keep one form across the games. Solo already has »Pijin / Pijine« (Sneg), so »Liine« → »Lijine« (also no »ii« for a 7-year-old to decode). Ask the producer whether SP's »Miin« type is wanted instead. — Skrivnostne sobe 27, 2026-11-19.
- A present »ima« followed by »Zdaj ga ni več« contradicts itself: the story's set-up takes the past (»je imel«). »zmagati vse igre« is a calque-like frame: »zmagati na turnirju / v igri«, or »dobiti igro«. Unconfirmed by a native. — Skrivnostne sobe 28, 26, 2026-11-19.
