## Lessons
- A running joke can live in the ending lines, which are not logic: a 7-year-old repeats a silly image (a child leaving the ball they sat on). Evidence: Kje je kdo? 4 and 12, Tea and Domen sit on the ball.
- Name one picturable thing in the story line (chocolate cake, a snowman waving); the bare fact feels like a list. Evidence: Kje je kdo? puzzles 1, 5, 11.
- Across a set, vary the culprit's reason: shy, lonely, brave, surprise-giving each repeat too often. Evidence: Kje je kdo? 3, 6, 7 are all »shy child«.
