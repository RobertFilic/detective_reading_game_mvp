# Lessons: book editor

Read before every task. Add up to 3 lessons after every task: the rule, then — evidence (what happened, where). A lesson confirmed twice moves up to **Rules**; a lesson proved wrong is deleted. Keep this file under ~60 lines. Playtest evidence beats review opinion.

## Rules
- A payoff needs its setup heard aloud first, in the calls: a silent bell lands only if it rang all week; »Igrače res hodijo same!« lands only if Ferdo grumbled »Igrače ne hodijo same!« before. Evidence: sneg n3/n6 (BIM-BAM heard once), igrace i1–i6 (the grumble never said, 2026-11-19).
- The season's thread (the snowman, the walking toys) is pictured in every call, one line, like muzej's »Spet čudne reči! Rex stoji na eni nogi.« Evidence: sneg n1, n4; igrace i2–i5 calls say nothing of the toys (2026-11-19).
- Across a set, vary the culprit's reason; no reason twice in one week, and check the previous batch too. Pick from: mix-up, keep it safe, give courage, help someone else, try it once, surprise. Evidence: Kje je kdo? 3, 6, 7 all »shy child«; rooms 17–27, ten of eleven hearts »secretly fixed it«, 25 repeats 18's »luknjo zašila« (2026-11-19).

## Lessons
- A running joke can live in the ending lines, which are not logic: a 7-year-old repeats a silly image (a child leaving the ball they sat on). Evidence: Kje je kdo? 4 and 12, Tea and Domen sit on the ball.
- Name one picturable thing in the story line (chocolate cake, a snowman waving); the bare fact feels like a list. Evidence: Kje je kdo? puzzles 1, 5, 11.
- The title and the win line must not give away the kind reason; »Kdo je popravil zvezdo?« spends the heart before the puzzle. Evidence: Kje je kdo? 24 (2026-11-19).
- When a grown-up takes a small child's thing, the child must have left it first (ran for ice cream), or it reads as a grab. Evidence: Kje je kdo? 28, Larisa and Nino's bucket (2026-11-19).
- A rival must speak in her own voice, with her own tic, in every case she appears. Evidence: sneg (review, no child yet): Ajda is the »worthy rival« but every line of hers is a flat third-person witness sentence; nothing to remember her by.
- The season's big answer must speak, with a tic, in cases 1–5 and in the final's scene; a silent culprit makes the finale a name, not a person. Evidence: igrace, Gal has one flat line (i2 s7) before the final and none in i6's scene.
