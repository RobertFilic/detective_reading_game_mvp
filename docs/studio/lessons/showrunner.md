# Lessons: showrunner

Read before every task. Add up to 3 lessons after every task: the rule, then — evidence (what happened, where). A lesson confirmed twice moves up to **Rules**; a lesson proved wrong is deleted. Keep this file under ~60 lines. Playtest evidence beats review opinion.

## Rules
- A big mystery made of small oddities works: one strange detail per case (a tiny green footprint, »pip-pip« from the vent, a missing spoon), each harmless alone, together pointing at one answer. — the 👾 Bip season; the producer praised the »red line« and the child enjoys it (PLAYTEST 2026-11-01).

## Lessons
- Hook the next case from the letter's P. S. and the detective's thought; it makes the season feel like one story. — Bip: »P. S. Iz kuhinje je izginil moj sir!« led into case 3.
- A puzzle can carry the thread across cases: the code 3-1-4 found in case 4 opens the lock in case 5. Plan such links in the pitch, and plan the fallback (the last hint gives the code). — Bip 4 → 5.
- Let the child commit to a theory halfway (the guess after case 3) and check it in the final case. Make the right answer reachable from the cards and the wrong options tempting. — Bip; to be confirmed by playtest.
- The mystery guest as a nameless suspect (👀 »???«) that turns into the real face on the solved screen gives the final case its reveal moment. Use the device once; next season find a different kind of reveal. — Bip 6.
- Rest overused culprits and motives: Tim was culprit in 4 of 12 space cases, and food was the motive in about a third of all cases. — LEDGER.md, 2026-11-01.
- In a season about lies, plan a lie map: who lies in each case, and make the liar innocent in at least 2 cases, so »whoever lies did it« never becomes the pattern. — galeb pitch (liar innocent in cases 3–5); to be confirmed by playtest.
- Plant the final's key claim as a harmless self-claim halfway (Vera's »Ob devetih že spim.« in case 3), next to a card that quietly contradicts it; break it only in the final. — galeb pitch; to be confirmed.
- Defuse the »never caught« tell by having two cast members who are never culprits of a small case, both tempting in the guess. — Bip 6 fair-play lesson, applied in the galeb pitch (Bepi and Vera).
