# Lessons: case writer

Read before every task. Add up to 3 lessons after every task: the rule, then — evidence (what happened, where). A lesson confirmed twice moves up to **Rules**; a lesson proved wrong is deleted. Keep this file under ~60 lines. Playtest evidence beats review opinion.

## Rules
- The call reads as one connected phone call, never three separate facts: each line follows from the last, every fact carries the caller's reason for saying it, and it ends with a request to the detective. Producer's rule. — pokal p1 call (2026-11-12): »Žoge imajo proge! / Pred slikanjem ob osmih so bile še bele. / Sodnica Neža vse zapiše v zvezek.« read »odd, unconnected«; now »Hej, pomagaj! Naše žoge imajo črte! / Ob osmih smo se slikali. Prej so bile še bele. / Sodnica Neža vse zapiše v zvezek. Poglej vanj!«
- Use the everyday word a 7-year-old says, not a precise or bookish one: »črte«, not »proge«. Producer's rule. — pokal p1 (2026-11-12).
- Every innocent suspect needs an alibi that names them and really makes it impossible; the culprit has none. If another sentence also truly clears them, mark it `clear` too (at most two), or the child who uses it is told they are wrong. The checker enforces the count, not the strength. — all Bip cases; g5 s11 (full-flow audit 2026-11-05).
- Say *when* the deed happened (call, question or first sentence: »Ponoči …«, »Po zajtrku … Zvečer …«), and make every alibi cover that whole time with the same time word. An alibi without a time, next to a decoy that says the suspect *could* do it, gives the case two answers. — Galeb 1 (playtest 2026-11-02), vesolje audit v2/v3/v5, Bip audit b3/b4/b5 (2026-11-05).
- A UGOVOR claim and its breaker share a time word, and the breaker must make »both true« impossible: »zjutraj pri svetilniku« vs »zjutraj na strehi« fails (a gull flies), »vse jutro pri svetilniku« works; »luč ponoči« fails (night light), »ponoči je tipkalo« works. — Galeb 2 plan, Galeb audit g3/g4/g6 (2026-11-05).

- Scene labels are one short word (»tla«, »vrata«, »listek«): a label that wraps makes its whole row of tiles taller. »pod stikalom« wrapped on the SE (m1); »oglasna deska« and »zadnja vrata« pushed m4's main button off the landscape phone with 10 objects (`shots.mjs` flagged it, fixed by one-word labels). — Muzej m1, m4.
- Count the sentences before writing: 3 alibis + 2 claims + breaker + proof + truth + card + a two-part bait is 11, level A's ceiling; a plant that doesn't fit goes into the reveal or letter (Rambo's »Aaauh!« in m5). — Galeb 1, muzej m5.

## Lessons
- Don't reach for »X ima (zelo) rad(a) Y« as the decoy or »vso noč« in alibis; they appear in most cases so far and a child can learn the pattern instead of reading. — LEDGER.md, 2026-11-01.
- A possessive form of a name must match the character's name pattern (`re`), or the checker can't see the name: Kiki's pattern needed `Kikin…` for »V Kikini kletki«. Check new forms like »Elina«, »Timovo«. — Bip 3.
- The corkboard card must name no suspect, and the proof must name only the culprit. A nameless culprit (Bip as »???«) is named by where it hides: its pattern is `zračnik…`, so only the proof may mention the vent in that case. — Bip 6.
- Write the excuse as the culprit's voice, short and funny; it is the line children repeat. — »Hotel sem samo en piškot … no, tri.« (Bip 1).
- The scene object's emoji is seen before any sentence is read: don't hide the proof behind the culprit's trademark thing (Luka's bucket 🪣); hide it behind the place (🔔 zvon). — Galeb 3.
- Before choosing an alibi, read the season's already-written cases: a frame used next door (»smrčal kot medved«, »z mamo«) is a repeat even in another case. And an alibi must not say something the season's truth makes false (Vera »asleep at dawn« would be false; »v sobi 7« is true). — Galeb 3.
- `words()` counts »…« and emoji as words, and a sentence is counted whole (not split) in `sentences`: »Ja … Na dimniku je gnezdo. Ne podrite ga!« is 9. — Galeb 4.
- Back an innocent's true UGOVOR claim with a non-suspect witness in their own voice (Ivo: »Ob osmih sem z Lanom odšel domov.«), not a narrator line that repeats the claim word for word; add a `hmm` for that pair (»To se ujema!«), so testing the true claim is never counted wrong. — muzej m5.
- When the deed leaves no trace (a shadow, a sound), give it a shape in the call (»po steni je hodil dinozaver«), so the proof's object (a costume tail) touches the deed and not only the place. — muzej m5.
- Keep each call line to about 35 characters: two lines that wrap push the main button off a landscape phone (`shots.mjs` flags it). — Galeb 1.
- In a step case with a proof pair, put the half that names the culprit first in `sentences`: the checker's solvability pass reads the first `evidence` as »the« proof and fails a nameless one. — Galeb 6 (the cipher half had to move after Hana's answer).
- A sentence's 8 words count across the whole text, quotes and »No ja …« included; only other lines (call, hints, letter) are split into sentences. A two-part truth like »No ja … X. Ampak Y!« usually needs cutting. — Galeb 5.
- The second, true UGOVOR claim can be the corkboard card (a quote with `who` that names no suspect): one sentence does two jobs and the case stays at 9. — Galeb 5.
- An innocent's own words (»Ves dan sem bila na vrtu.«) are no alibi: the game itself says »To trdi o sebi«. Back it with a witness or a narrated fact, above all when the scene confirms that suspect's decoy (Maja likes the Moon + a new Moon photo). — vesolje v5 audit.
- Pin the *start* of the deed's window, not only the end: »Kamera je zjutraj izginila« plus a decoy »had it yesterday« lets the deed slip into the night, before the »ves dan« alibis. »Po zajtrku je … izginila« closes it. — vesolje v5.
- A science fact in the case (»V vesolju voda ne teče, ampak lebdi«) applies to every liquid in it: a sweaty decoy next to it makes the floating drops ambiguous. Keep decoy liquids soaked in (»mokra majica«). — vesolje v2 audit.
- In an ask step, put the time in the *answer* (»Ob polnoči sva z Elo …«), never only in the `ask`: the solve screen shows just »Tim: answer«. — Bip 2 audit.
- A »can't do it« alibi must close every way in that the case itself shows: »can't open the lock« fails when the room has a vent. One sentence that shuts all routes is better (»do jutra v zaklenjeni kletki«). — Bip 5 audit.
- Let the proof touch the deed's own trace, not only the culprit: »Med vijaki leži Timov copat« ties Tim to the crash; »Timovo darilo za škatlami« only showed he had been there at some time. — Bip 5 audit.
- A puzzle's sentence can't easily take a time word (cipher: ≤ 9 different letters, ≤ 20 letters; a geslo is fixed by its grid). Time it from another on-screen sentence that repeats its words: »Ponoči je Luka z mano lovil ribe.« (Bepi) times the cipher »Bepi je lovil ribe.« — Galeb audit g2.
- Solve crosses off only with the `clear` sentence. A neutral breaker that plainly clears (»Ob zori je Bepi plaval v bazenu«) gets a »wrong« when the child uses it; make the breaker a trace (»Ob zori so bili Bepijevi rokavčki mokri«) and keep the plain alibi in one sentence. — Galeb audit g3.
- A trace decoy (a feather on the castle) needs an on-screen sentence that times it (»Zjutraj je Gusti pristala na podrtem gradu.«), not only the reveal. And don't let the culprit or the liar give an innocent's alibi: a non-suspect in a scene quote (Rozi at 🛎️ recepcija) is trusted. — Galeb audit g5, g3.
- Run `node tools/shots.mjs <id>` before reporting. A scene object's emoji must not look like a game tool: a 🔦 object sat right under the »🔦 Svetilka« button. — Muzej m1.
- In a running countdown, a letter says the *next* day's number (it arrives on the next visit): m1's letter »Še štiri dni«, m2's »Še tri dni«. I first wrote today's »Še dva dneva« in m4's letter. Read the neighbours' letters before writing yours. — Muzej m4.
- A »too high for the animal« alibi fails at an ordinary height, because children know cats jump. Close the way the animal could really do it: »Staro stikalo je pretrdo za Fosilove tačke.« (instead of the plan's »previsoko«). — Muzej m1.
- Contrast the decoy's time with the proof's freshness, so one reading settles both: »pozabil že zjutraj« (old) against »nova Lanova nalepka« (new). The proof then touches the deed's time, not only the place. — Muzej m1.
- The checker finds names only in a sentence's *text*, never in `who`: the culprit's own »Izgubila sem en rdeč copat.« names nobody, so the naming half of a pair needs the name written in it (a witness: »Mila je izgubila …«). The same goes for kin words: »dedkov apčiii« matched Dedek, not Ivo; write »dedek Ivo«. — Muzej m6.
- In a case with no scene, every narrated clue in the plan (»Na Rexovem zobu visi …«) has to be somebody's answer. Count the speakers first: every suspect (even the cat: »Mijav! Prrr …«) and every witness needs an answer, and m6 came to exactly 11. — Muzej m6.
- When the ask count runs over 11, give a required answer a second job: the culprit's answer can be the 🧭 clue (Zoja: »Mijav! Po odru so mačje tačke. Fosil?«), and the animal's answer can be the proof if its text names only the culprit (Fosil: »Mrrr!« Prinese Zojin mačji copat …; the speaker sits in `who`, not in the text). m2 went from 12 to 11 with no plant moved. — Muzej m2.
- A plant that makes the trace impossible for someone (»Rambo ima velike škornje« against paws »velike kot otroški čevelj«) truly clears them: mark it `clear` as their second alibi, or a careful child is told they are wrong. — Muzej m2.
- Count the askers before placing alibis: every suspect and every witness must answer in `ask`, so with 5 people an asker's one answer should do two jobs (Rambo's yawn + Zoja's alibi: »Aaauh! Ob sedmih je bila Zoja v garderobi.«). That kept the case at 10 sentences. — muzej m3.
- In a sighting claim, put the place after the person seen (»videl Ramba pri vitrini«). »Sem pri vitrini videl Ramba« can be read as the speaker's own place, and then his alibi elsewhere breaks the claim too, so there are two breakers. Add a `hmm` for that alibi (»Od kita se vidi do vitrine.«). — muzej m3.
- `shots.mjs` can't see a case whose file has a future `opens` date (its cases are dropped before the page starts): run a scratchpad copy that fakes `Date` to the release day (and points ROOT at the repo). — pokal p3 (»✘ no case p3«).
- A suspect's own plant about a *neighbouring* time is safe beside a witness alibi for the deed's time, and it teaches the rung: Tone »Med tekmami delam v kleti.« next to Neža »Med odmorom sva s Tonetom popravljala mali gol.« Check the two can both be true (half-time is not between matches). — pokal p3.
- A 4-word window (»od enih do dveh«) + »je« + a name leaves two words: a past verb plus a place (»je bil Tone v kleti«) is 9. Fit it with verb + object (»je Luka trdno spal«), a witness's »sem + dative« (»sem Hani pletla kitke«) or a sound (»brusil: bzzz!«), and let the object's `label` (»klet«) give the place. — pokal p5.
- A time-sighting proof that contradicts the liar's claim is a second UGOVOR breaker. Pick a proof the claim could live with (a referee *takes a ball* at one: fine if there was a match), so only the planned breaker (the urnik) breaks it, and add a `hmm` for that pair. — pokal p5 (the plan's »Od enih je Neža streljala na gol« would have broken »Od enih sem že sodila tekmo«).
- The game bolds names in the sentence text, not in the speaker label. If the culprit only speaks elsewhere, the proof is the only bold culprit name. Name them in someone else's quote that proves nothing (Zoja: »Profesor Ivo zobu pravi: moj lepotec!«). — muzej m3 screenshots.
- Put the culprit's wrong-time »alibi« in someone else's mouth (Neža: »Pisk! Pred slikanjem je Tone čistil fotoaparat.«), not the culprit's own: it looks like a real alibi (no »To trdi o sebi«), the ⏰ step yellow-cards it, and it names the culprit outside the proof, so the proof is not the only bold »Tone«. — pokal p6.
- `shots.mjs` can't see a case whose file has a future `opens` (»✘ no case p6«). Run a scratchpad copy of it with a `Date` shim (`evaluateOnNewDocument`) set to the release day; don't edit the tool. — pokal p6.
- A score in a call (»2 : 0«) breaks across lines at its spaces: »Gooool! Ježki so zmagali 2 : 0!« wrapped as »2 / : 0!« on the iPhone 14. Give a code-carrying score its own short call line (≤ 25 characters). — pokal p4 shots.
- Shots of a file with a future `opens` say »no case«: the game drops it until the date. Run a scratch copy of `shots.mjs` with `Date` faked past `opens` and the »Razumem« coach card clicked away, or the coach hides the scene. — pokal p4.
- An alibi's place must not share a word with the deed's place: »Pia je stala v golu« reads as »in the goal« of a case about a secret goal, and »ob igrišču« could be the small pitch. Set it against the deed's place (»na velikem igrišču« vs »mali gol«). — pokal p2.
- A proof that would make a child tell on a sibling (Pia on Bor) goes to a non-suspect witness with a reason to see it: Tone, who mends the nets, »Kos mreže malega gola visi na Borovem rogu.« Kinder, and trusted. — pokal p2.
- Check the call's story time against the film and the ⏰ cards: a call »during the match« can't come with a film frame »Po tekmi …«. Make the call time-neutral (no score, no »še traja«) and report the clash. — pokal p2.
- A group word in an alibi can clear the culprit too: »Ob osmih je Tone slikal vse v avli« reads as »everyone, Zoja included, was in the hall«. Name the thing instead (»slikal pokal«). — pokal p1.
- To name the culprit outside the proof, pair them with the decoy suspect in a line with no hour (»Luka in Zoja vpijeta: »Ta pokal bo naš!««): it plants a running line and proves nothing. With no time word, nobody can read it as an alibi. — pokal p1.
- With six writers in one file, `shots.mjs` can say »no case« while someone else is half-way through an edit. Run it again before you look for a bug. — pokal p1.
- When a plan detail changes (a colour, a team name), grep the whole `index.html` for the old sentence: the UI style-guide mock (`ui-row` samples) repeats p1's proof line, and scores use non-breaking spaces (»2 : 0«), so a plain-space search misses them. — pokal real-clubs revision.
- Real stars (Yamal, Kane) stay in excuses, letters and `think` lines, never in a clue: the checker sees no name pattern for them, so only your own care keeps them out of evidence and alibis. — pokal real-clubs revision.
