# Lessons: case writer

Read before every task. Add up to 3 lessons after every task: the rule, then — evidence (what happened, where). A lesson confirmed twice moves up to **Rules**; a lesson proved wrong is deleted. Keep this file under ~60 lines. Playtest evidence beats review opinion.

## Rules
- Every innocent suspect needs exactly one alibi that names them and really makes it impossible; the culprit has none. The checker enforces the count, not the strength. — all Bip cases.

## Lessons
- Don't reach for »X ima (zelo) rad(a) Y« as the decoy or »vso noč« in alibis; they appear in most cases so far and a child can learn the pattern instead of reading. — LEDGER.md, 2026-11-01.
- A possessive form of a name must match the character's name pattern (`re`), or the checker can't see the name: Kiki's pattern needed `Kikin…` for »V Kikini kletki«. Check new forms like »Elina«, »Timovo«. — Bip 3.
- The corkboard card must name no suspect, and the proof must name only the culprit. A nameless culprit (Bip as »???«) is named by where it hides: its pattern is `zračnik…`, so only the proof may mention the vent in that case. — Bip 6.
- Write the excuse as the culprit's voice, short and funny; it is the line children repeat. — »Hotel sem samo en piškot … no, tri.« (Bip 1).
- The scene object's emoji is seen before any sentence is read: don't hide the proof behind the culprit's trademark thing (Luka's bucket 🪣); hide it behind the place (🔔 zvon). — Galeb 3.
- Before choosing an alibi, read the season's already-written cases: a frame used next door (»smrčal kot medved«, »z mamo«) is a repeat even in another case. And an alibi must not say something the season's truth makes false (Vera »asleep at dawn« would be false; »v sobi 7« is true). — Galeb 3.
- `words()` counts »…« and emoji as words, and a sentence is counted whole (not split) in `sentences`: »Ja … Na dimniku je gnezdo. Ne podrite ga!« is 9. — Galeb 4.
- A claim and its breaker must use the same time word (»Ves večer …« / »Zvečer …«); »ponoči« against »ob desetih« lets a careful reader say »both can be true«. — Galeb 2, the plan's draft.
- Count the sentences before writing: 3 alibis + claim + breaker + a separate proof + truth + card + decoy is 9 in a scene case; the plan's »7« assumed the breaker is the proof. — Galeb 1.
- Keep each call line to about 35 characters: two lines that wrap push the main button off a landscape phone (`shots.mjs` flags it). — Galeb 1.
- In a step case with a proof pair, put the half that names the culprit first in `sentences`: the checker's solvability pass reads the first `evidence` as »the« proof and fails a nameless one. — Galeb 6 (the cipher half had to move after Hana's answer).
- A sentence's 8 words count across the whole text, quotes and »No ja …« included; only other lines (call, hints, letter) are split into sentences. A two-part truth like »No ja … X. Ampak Y!« usually needs cutting. — Galeb 5.
- The second, true UGOVOR claim can be the corkboard card (a quote with `who` that names no suspect): one sentence does two jobs and the case stays at 9. — Galeb 5.
