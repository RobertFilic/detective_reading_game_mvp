# Playtest notes

What real children did. This is the strongest evidence the studio has: it beats any review or opinion. The producer adds an entry after playing; the agents read it before every season and in every retro.

Write what you **saw and heard**, not only whether it was good. Useful things to note:
- Where the child laughed, said »aha!«, or talked about the story away from the screen.
- Where they hesitated, guessed, asked for help, or wanted to stop.
- Which hint they reached (the adult debrief shows hints and assisted solves per case).
- What they asked for next.

Names are not written here: use »child 1«, »child 2« and an age.
