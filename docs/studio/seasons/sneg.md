## 1. Title, place, level, rung

- **Title:** ❄️ *Sneženi možje brez sledi*. Board question (already the teaser): **»Kdo ponoči dela snežene može?«** The question is a trap on purpose: nobody makes them at night (§2).
- **Place:** **Penzion pri Smreki**, a wooden guesthouse in the mountain village **Smrečje**, in the winter holidays. Pokal's promise comes true: »Pozimi gremo vsi na sneg!« Lan, Zoja, Luka, Hana, Pia and Bor stay a week. The places: the hall with Ajda's weather board (*tabla*) and the coat hooks, the front door, the kitchen, the **woodshed** (*drvarnica*, two doors: one into the house, one to the front yard), the orchard of young trees, the sled hill, the village square, the pond, the igloo behind the house.
- **Shape:** six mornings, Monday to Saturday, one case a day (days in sequence, as in 🦔; no one-day clock). Every morning there is fresh snow and **a new snowman somewhere in the village, with no footprints around it.**
- **Level A** (»Stopnja A«): ≤ 8 words a sentence, 4 suspects, 7–9 sentences (final 10–11), names only.
- **Reading knob: negation, level 1** (»ni«, »nihče ni«, »nič«, »brez«, »nobene«). One negation a sentence, early in it, never doubled. The clue's negation is the sentence's 1st–3rd word wherever the clue hangs on it. Cases 1–2's calls end with **»Išči, česar ni!«** (3).
- **Rung 5: »What did *not* happen.«** The ladder:
  1. Case 1, **shown**: one object lacks what all the others have (a sled with no snow on it). It is the proof.
  2. Case 2, **heard**: a witness says what someone did *not* do (»Zoja ni šla iz kuhinje«). It is an alibi.
  3. Case 3, **an absence breaks a lie** (❗ UGOVOR): »I went out at night« vs no footprints in front of the door.
  4. Case 4, **no trace of a person at all** → nobody did it (»Kaj …?«). The proof is a bare roof.
  5. Case 5, **the dog that didn't bark**: Bor bleats at everyone, and this afternoon he never bleated. A pair.
  6. Final, **needed**: two absences turn the season's own question around (not at night, and not through the door).
- **Old skills mixed in:** crossing off every case (1); the corkboard and two guesses (2); ❗ once, an innocent liar (3); 🧭 once, in the final (4); a time window with two edges in the final (6).

## 2. The big mystery and the true answer

- **As a child would say it:** »Vsako jutro stoji v vasi nov snežak. Okoli njega ni nobene stopinje. Kdo jih dela ponoči?«
- **The true answer: Ajda**, 8, Cilka's granddaughter, who lives at the guesthouse. Every morning she writes on the hall board when the snow will start tonight (»Nocoj sneži od devetih.«). Every evening she carries firewood in from the woodshed. She reads detective books. On the first evening Luka bragged »Naš detektiv reši vse!«, and Ajda bet him she could make a mystery the famous detective (the child, from the newspapers) could not solve: **snowmen without footprints.**
- **How:** every evening, carrying wood, she slipped out of the woodshed's yard door (never the front door), built one snowman shortly **before the snow she had forecast**, and was back inside before it started. The night's snow covered her footprints. **Nobody made them at night**, so the board's question was the trap.
- **Why it is fair (the chain):** no footprints (card 1) + a thick cap of fresh snow on the hat (card 2) → it stood there *before* the snow fell, so in the evening. Who knows exactly when the snow starts? Ajda (her board, case 1 on). Nobody opened the front door at night (card 3) → whoever it was went out another way, earlier. The snowmen's arms are kindling from the woodshed (card 4) → whoever goes to the woodshed in the evening: Ajda (case 3 plant). The last one holds a magnifier made of ice (card 5) → someone playing detective: Ajda reads detective books (case 4 plant).
- **Why the tempting ones are innocent:** **Marko** ploughs the road at night (out at night, from outside the house: cards 3 and 4 seem to fit him), but the snowmen were made before the snow, and he only ploughs once it has fallen. In the evenings he plays cards with Cilka. **Luka** is always first in the snow (case 1) and the detective's biggest fan (card 5), but he never goes out at night (his own tall tale breaks in case 3), and in the evening he plays memory with Lan.
- **Motive:** **to win a bet** and to test the famous detective. Not a gift, not help for everyone. Funny: she loses, and pulls Luka up the sled hill on her sled, as they bet.
- **The warm ending:** on Saturday Ajda builds a seventh snowman *with* the children, in daylight, **with footprints everywhere** (»Tokrat s stopinjami!«). The only child in Smrečje in winter now has six friends and one rival detective. Newspaper: »SNEŽNE NOVICE«. `answer`: ⛄ »Snežake je delala Ajda. Zvečer, preden je snežilo!« (4 + 4)
- **Wink for grown-ups:** *Murder on the Orient Express* (no tracks in the snow: the culprit is inside) and *Silver Blaze* (the dog in the night: Bor's silence).
