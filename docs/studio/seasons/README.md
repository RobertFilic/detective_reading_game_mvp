# Season pitches

One file per season, `<id>.md`. The editorial director opens it with the season brief, the showrunner pitches inside it, and the producer approves both before any case is written. The puzzle designer then adds the puzzles under each case, and the file stays the season's working plan.

The file has these parts (see [the showrunner's brief](../../../.claude/agents/showrunner.md)):
0. **Season brief** (editorial director): rung, reading knob, feeling, must-haves, what to avoid, ship date
1. Title, place, level and reading knobs, the skill rung
2. The big mystery and its true answer
3. The red thread: the card each case pins, the guess and its options
4. Six cases, one line each: question, culprit, motive, proof, decoy, steps, the skill's moment, **and the skeleton** (below)
5. What is new (mechanic, structure, feeling) and what is deliberately not repeated
6. Cast: returning and new, one trait each
7. Risks
8. **Status:** briefed → pitched → approved (date) → written → reviewed → playtested
9. **Decisions:** one line per clash or 🟠 finding that was not fixed: date, what, why, who (docs/studio/README.md, »How the agents work together«)

## The skeleton: the season without its language

Section 4 holds each case's logic as **facts in English**, apart from any Slovenian draft line, so the case can later be rewritten in another language instead of translated:

| Case | Deed (what, when, where) | Culprit, motive | Proof (the fact) | Alibis (suspect: the fact that makes it impossible) | Decoy (the fact) | Card (the fact) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 *(example)* | the cookie jar was emptied, in the evening, in the kitchen | A, saving cookies for a friend's party | A's pocket is full of crumbs | B: in the bath all evening (a parent saw) · C: too small to reach the shelf | B loves cookies | a crumb trail that leads to the window |

Slovenian draft lines may follow the table. A fact that only works through Slovenian wording (a past-tense gender ending, a word puzzle, a pun) is marked **language-bound** ([languages/sl.md §8](../languages/sl.md)), so another language knows to rebuild it.
