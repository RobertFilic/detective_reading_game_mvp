## 1. Title, place, level, rung

- **Title:** ⚽ *Skrivnost zlatega pokala*. Board question and teaser (already in `COMING`): **»Kam je izginil zlati pokal?«**
- **Place:** the school playground, the gym and the hall of the host school on the day of the **Turnir prijateljstva**, a friendship tournament for four invited teams. This is the invitation Lan got in the museum's last letter (»P. S. Lan je dobil vabilo na nogometni turnir!«). The places are the hall (*avla*) with the trophy cabinet (*vitrina*), the big pitch with two benches, the small pitch behind the gym with a spare goal, the dining room, the gym roof, and the cellar with the caretaker's workshop.
- **The whole season is one day.** This is the new shape. Saturday, from eight in the morning to the five o'clock final. Every case takes place one step later on the same clock, and the corkboard is the day's timetable (§6.3). The **urnik** (timetable) is a real clue all season:

| Ura | Kaj | Case |
|---|---|---|
| ob osmih | slikanje s pokalom v avli (the group photo with the cup) | 1 |
| ob devetih | 1. polfinale: 🔵 Barcelona : ⚪ Real → **3 : 1** | 2 |
| ob desetih | 2. polfinale: 🩵 City : 🔴 Bayern → **2 : 0** | 3 |
| opoldne | kosilo (lunch, until one) | 4 |
| ob dveh | tekma za tretje mesto: Real : Bayern | 5 |
| ob petih | finale: Barcelona : City | 6 ⭐ |

- **Level:** A (first line on the board: »Stopnja A«). At most 8 words a sentence, 4 suspects a case, 7–9 sentences (the final 10–11).
- **Reading knob raised (only one): time words**, one notch past the museum's clock hours. The museum had *ob šestih, ob osmih*. This season adds the **relative** words:
  - cases 1–2: clock hours (known) and *pred / med / po* + one noun (*pred tekmo, med tekmo, po tekmi*);
  - cases 3–6: *preden, medtem ko, šele*, and a window *od … do …* / *po … do …* (*od enih do dveh*, *po slikanju do devetih*).
  - Every time clause fits in 8 words. The drafts below prove it can be done. No other knob moves: names only, negation rare, *who speaks* stays at Galeb's level.
- **Skill rung 6: »When, exactly?«** A true alibi for the wrong time is no alibi. Being at the place at the wrong time is no guilt. Put things in order by the clock and by *preden / potem*. What is new this season:
  1. **The time of a sentence is a thing you check**, with its own step and reward: ⏰ »Kdaj?« sorts sentences into PREJ / TAKRAT / POTEM, and a wrong-time sentence gets the referee's yellow card 🟨 »NAPAČEN ČAS!« (§6.1).
  2. **The skill works both ways, every case:** an innocent seen *at the place* at the wrong time (not guilty), and someone *away from the place* at the wrong time (not cleared). Each case has at least one of each (§5.1).
  3. **A deed's time can be read from the timetable.** In case 5 a lie breaks because the urnik says the first match after lunch was at two.
  4. **A window with two edges** in the final (after the photo, by nine): an alibi must cover the whole window.
- **The rung order is swapped with rung 5.** The ladder lets neighbours swap. The snow season one week later (❄️ »Sneženi možje brez sledi«) fits rung 5 (*what did not happen*: no footprints). So pokal uses **no absence clue**. Zoja is missing from the eight o'clock photo, but that is never the proof.
- **Old skills mixed in:** crossing off in every case (rung 1); the corkboard and two guesses (rung 2); ❗ UGOVOR once, in case 5 (rung 3); 🧭 »Kam kaže sled?« once, in case 3, with a *time* explanation (rung 4).

## 2. The big mystery and the true answer

- **The big mystery, as a child would say it:** »Zlati pokal je izginil. Pred vsako tekmo pa se pojavi čudna risba. Kdo je vzel pokal?«
- **Background:** at eight the four teams take a photo in the hall with the golden cup; Luka holds it. Soon after, the cabinet is empty. The tournament goes on, because the final is not until five. And before the matches, a strange grey drawing turns up on a bench: a team's name in **white letters on grey**. First BARCELONA, then REAL, CITY, BAYERN, and at last all four together. Nobody says they drew them.
- **The true answer:** **hišnik Tone**, the school caretaker. He made every metal sign on the school's doors. Right after the photo he carried the cup to his cellar workshop in its box. He wanted to **engrave the name of every team** on it, so that whoever wins the final, every child is on the golden cup. After each name he checked his letters the way you check a coin: paper on the cup, rub with his thick pencil. **The strange drawings are those rubbings.** The cut letters stay white and the gold around them goes grey. He left each one on that team's bench before a match, »za srečo« (for luck). He never signed them, because he wanted to keep the surprise.
- **Why it is fair (the clue chain the child can follow):**
  1. In case 1 the child **makes a rubbing themselves** with the new 🖍️ pencil and sees white letters come out of grey (§6.2). From then on the drawings look like rubbings. Card 2's thought says so: »Bele črke na sivem? Kot moje drgnjenje!«
  2. A rubbing shows letters that are **cut into something**. Card 3 shows the outline of a **handle**, so that something is the cup. Card 4 shows **golden crumbs**, so the cutting is fresh and in gold.
  3. Who cuts letters into metal? »Napise na vratih šole je vrezal hišnik Tone.« (case 1). Who is at the benches before every match? Tone: »Pred vsako tekmo postavim klopi.« (case 2). Who is in the cellar all day while a buzzing comes from there? Tone (his alibis in cases 3 and 5, the 🔊 »bzzz!« on the corkboard).
  4. Card 5: **all four names** on one drawing. Whoever made them cheers for everyone, not for one team.
- **Why the tempting ones are innocent:**
  - **Luka** held the cup at the photo and says »Ta pokal bo naš!«. But he held it *before* and *at* the photo, and the cup vanished *after* it. From the photo to nine he was running laps. Also, a City fan wouldn't wish BARCELONA luck.
  - **Zoja** draws everything (green teeth on the balls in case 1). But her drawings are coloured, and these are grey rubbings. From the photo to nine she was washing the balls with Lan.
- **The warm ending:**
  - **The cup comes back** (a new kind of reveal, after Bip's »???«, Galeb's book and the museum's slipper). At five, Tone walks onto the pitch with the cup under a cloth. He pulls the cloth away and the names light up one by one, »bzzz«: BARCELONA · REAL · CITY · BAYERN. The crowd cheers. Last, a line lights up with the child's name from the ID card, on the device only (the producer, 2026-11-06).
  - Tone: »Bo že, bo! Vsaka ekipa mora na pokal.«
  - The final is played; the winner lifts the cup, and every team's name is on it. The newspaper (as built) carries the child's name: the detective who found the golden cup.
- **A wink for the grown-ups:** the timetable mystery (*4.50 from Paddington*, *Five Little Pigs*) with no crime. The »thief« is the kindest man in the school.

## 6. What is new

### 6.1 New mechanic: ⏰ »Kdaj?« with the yellow card 🟨 (step `when`, right before `solve`)

The main session builds it on the torn/timeline tap-to-place code plus the stamp of UGOVOR and 🧭. Never in the same case as ❗ or 🧭.
- **What the child sees:** a time strip with three boxes: ⏪ **PREJ** · ⏰ **TAKRAT** · ⏩ **POTEM**. Over TAKRAT sits the deed's own card, with the question's time words (»⏰ ob osmih: žoge dobijo zobe«). Below are 2–4 sentence cards from the pocket, each naming one person and one time.
- **What the child taps:** a card, then a box.
- **The catch:** a card that lands in PREJ or POTEM gets the referee's **yellow card** 🟨 »NAPAČEN ČAS!« with a whistle »pisk!«, and it slides down. A card in TAKRAT glows green: »To šteje!«. When all are placed, a green card says »Zdaj veš, kaj šteje.« and »Vem, kdo je bil!« opens.
- **Wrong tries and the ladder:** »Poglej, kdaj je to bilo.« (5) → `h1` (the case's own) → the time words in the card light up → the right box pulses (assisted).
- **In solve:** a 🟨 card keeps a small 🟨 tag. Used to cross someone off, or against a face as proof, it says »Napačen čas! Poglej uro še enkrat.« (2 + 4), counted at level 0.
- **Without the step (case 5):** `when: 'after'` (or `'before'`) on a sentence gives the same solve note. The debrief counts »n× napačen čas«, the season's measure, as »očiten osumljenec« was the museum's.

- **Checker:** every card has a time word; the deed's words are in the question; a `during` card that puts an innocent elsewhere is their `clear`; no `during` card puts the culprit at the place (that would be the proof handed over); across the season the culprit is a card in at least half the `when` cases.

### 6.2 New tool: 🖍️ the pencil (»Podrgni!«)

A scene object with `rub: true` needs the pencil, the way a `dark` one needs the torch: tap 🖍️, then the object. Grey strokes sweep over the paper and the pressed letters come out **white**. ## 8. Risks and how the season avoids them

- **Relative time words are hard at 7** (*preden* reads like *pred*, and *potem ko* is long): cases 1–2 use hours and *pred / med / po* + a noun. Each clause has one event the child has lived through (the photo, the match, lunch). The ⏰ strip turns the words into three boxes they can see.
- **The 8-word limit with clauses:** every draft above is counted. If a writer can't fit one, the clause becomes a noun phrase (*pred tekmo*), not a ninth word.
- **»Wrong time« becomes the trick, not the skill** (»every sentence with another hour is useless«): case 1's wrong-time sentence is a strong temptation that must be *read*. Case 5's breaker is a time from the timetable. Case 6 has two TAKRAT readings, and only one is at the place. The child still crosses everyone off with alibis.
- **The step hands over the answer:** no `during` card ever puts the culprit at the place. The culprit is in the step in three of four cases, always at a wrong time (§5.1).
- **The rubbing isn't understood:** the child makes one in case 1, before any card, and card 2's thought names it. A child who misses it still has the handle, the gold, the cellar and the signs on the doors.
- **Too many returning names for a new player:** each one has one trait in their first case, names are bold, and the season stands alone. The producer chose the crossover cast (2026-11-06).
- **No animals as suspects, culprits or witnesses** (the producer, 2026-11-06). The old goat case becomes a little boy in a goat costume, so the white blur on the film and the net on the horn still work, and the costume keeps the joke. Bor is the youngest suspect: his motive is to play once, nobody tells him off, and he gets the mascot medal. Pia speaks about her brother only with a happy true fact (»plesal ob igrišču«), never as the proof.
- **A grown-up takes something without asking:** Tone is staff, the cup is back before the final, and his letter line is »Naslednjič povem vsaj Neži.« Neža laughs at her own rule-breaking. Both are warm, never told off.
- **The guess tell:** the right option moves to the middle (§3).
- **The final is long** (call, ask, lock, cellar, ⏰, solve: 6 screens; Muzej's had 5). The lock is quick, and the cellar has only 3 objects. If the playtest shows tiredness, the ask can move into the cellar scene as narrator lines.
- **Build time** (pushed by 2026-11-10; the producer chose to build ⏰ and 🖍️): ⏰ M, 🖍️ S, urnik board S, cup reveal S, `when` note S. Fallbacks, only if the build slips: without ⏰, cases 1, 2, 4 and 6 keep the 🟨 note in solve only (`when` marks), as case 5. Without 🖍️, a dark object and the torch (the drawings still say »bele črke«). Without the urnik board, the normal corkboard.
