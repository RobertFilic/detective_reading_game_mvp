# 🦕 Duh v muzeju (season 3): pitch

**Status:** pitched (2026-11-05) → **approved (2026-11-05, see §11)**

> All Slovenian in this file is a draft for the case writers. A native speaker checks it before it goes into the game. Draft lines were counted the checker's way (emoji, »…« and »1-2-3« count as words); the writers count again.

## 1. Title, place, level, rung

- **Title:** 🦕 *Duh v muzeju*. Board question: **»Kdo ponoči premika dinozavra?«**
- **Place:** the old natural-history museum in town, in the week before its **100th birthday** (Saturday, ob petih). The halls: the dinosaur hall at the very back with **Rex**, the T-rex skeleton; the whale hall with the whale skeleton hanging from the ceiling; the mammoth hall; the butterfly room; the crystal cabinet; the cloakroom; the little room by the front door where the night guard sits; the back door with its key hook.
- **The rhythm of the week:** every evening from six to eight the children's **Dino klub** goes on a torch walk through the museum (real museums run these). The small cases happen in those evenings, with grown-ups around. After eight the children go home. **At night only the night guard is in the museum**, and every morning Rex stands in a new pose. Every case opens with the morning phone call: what Rex is doing today, and what happened last evening.
- **Level:** A (first line on the board: »Stopnja A«). ≤ 8 words a sentence, 4 suspects a case, 7–9 sentences (the final 10–11).
- **Reading knob raised (only one): time words.** Galeb raised *who speaks*; it stays where it is (witnesses and claims), it does not grow. This season the deed has a **clock time** for the first time:
  - cases 1–2 stay with *zvečer / ves večer* (gentle, the Galeb fix pattern);
  - cases 3–6 use whole hours from the big museum clock: *ob šestih, ob sedmih, ob osmih, ob polnoči*. The call names the hour, and every alibi uses **the same hour word** (case-writer lesson: »ponoči« against »ob desetih« lets a careful reader say »both can be true«);
  - the final's puzzle orders the week by day names (*v ponedeljek … v soboto*).
  - No *preden / medtem ko / šele ko* yet: that is rung 6. Negation stays rare; every explanation is written in the positive.
- **Skill rung 4: the obvious suspect is innocent** (confirmed against CURRICULUM.md). A clue that points at someone **too clearly** was misread, left there by chance, or put there by someone else by accident. What is new this season:
  1. **Seeing through a clue** is an action with its own reward: the arrow that points at an innocent face bends away (the new step 🧭, §6).
  2. The clue is always **true**; only its meaning is wrong (a torch that was forgotten there in the morning, paw prints too big for a cat). This is different from Galeb's lie, where the sentence itself was false.
  3. **A witness can be sure and still be wrong** (case 3: he saw a shadow and named the guard). Here the old skill UGOVOR serves the new one.
  4. **Not every pointing clue is wrong** (case 4: two clues point, one is a real trace). So the lesson is »check why it points«, never »the obvious one is always innocent«.
  5. **The obvious suspect of the big mystery is Rado**, all season. In the small cases the »obvious« one rotates (Rado, the cat, the professor, a witness's word), so »never Rado« is not the pattern of the small cases.
- **Old skills mixed in:** crossing off with alibis in every case (rung 1); the corkboard and the guess (rung 2); ❗ UGOVOR in cases 3 and 5 (rung 3), each time where the misleading thing is a *sentence someone says*.

## 2. The big mystery and the true answer

- **The big mystery, as a child would say it:** »Vsako jutro Rex stoji drugače. Kdo ponoči premika dinozavra?«
- **Background:** Rex is not made of real bones: he is a light cast, and his bones move on joints (said in case 2). On Monday morning he is bowing. On Tuesday he stands on one leg. On Wednesday his tail is up. Every morning, a new pose, and nobody admits anything. Everyone looks at the grumpy night guard **Rado**: he is the only one in the museum at night, he has all the keys on his belt, a big torch, heavy boots, and he yawns all day.
- **The true answer:** **Mila**, the quiet museum guide. For the museum's 100th birthday she is preparing a surprise, **Dinoples** (the dinosaur dance), danced *with* Rex. She is too shy to practise by day, in front of visitors. So at midnight she lets herself in through the back door with the **spare key from the hook**, puts on her soft red dance slippers, hums »la-la-la«, marks her steps on the floor with chalk (»1-2-3«, the way she counts for her school groups), and dances with Rex. Every night she leaves him in the last step she practised. The poses, in the order of the week, **are the dance**.
- **Why Rado was innocent all along:** at night he sits in his room by the front door, far from the dinosaur hall at the back; the only camera is the one at the front door; he uses his own keys and never the spare one; his boots go »klop-klop«, not »tip-tip-tip«; and his cap on Rex's tooth (the final's strongest clue against him) is there because every evening he hangs it on Rex and says »Lahko noč, Rex.« The grumpy guard has a soft heart.
- **The warm ending:**
  - **The slipper moment** (a new kind of reveal, after Bip's »???« and Galeb's book cover): the child taps the little red slipper found behind Rex, it flies to Mila's foot, and it fits. ✨ (Pepelka, for detectives.)
  - Mila blushes: »Hotela sem presenetiti vse za rojstni dan.«
  - Saturday ob petih: the birthday show. Mila dances Dinoples with Rex, the whole Dino klub joins in, and the last one on the floor is **Rado**, who said all week »Jaz že ne plešem!«. He dances.
  - The newspaper (as built) with the child's name: the detective who cleared the night guard.
- **A wink for the grown-ups:** *Night at the Museum* without the magic: everything that seemed alive has a person and a kind reason behind it.

## 5. Fair-play check: the producer's seven rules, case by case

The culprit has none. The 🧭 explanation is never counted as an alibi: it explains a clue, it does not cross anyone off.

### 5.3 Rule 3: every clue is on screen, nothing only in the reveal or a P. S.

- Each proof is a sentence the child reads in that case (table §4). The proof pair of the final is two sentences of the final itself; card 5 is repeated there as »Za Rexom je spet ležal rdeč copat.«, so a child who forgot case 5 is not stuck.
- The reveals only retell. Things the reveal *adds* (Zoja borrowed the torch; tall Ivo made the shadow) are never needed to solve.
- No P. S. carries a clue; the guess after case 3 needs only cases 1–3 and their cards.
- The final's 🕐 puzzle uses the poses from the six calls; its last hint gives the order, so a child who doesn't remember the calls is never blocked.

### 5.4 Rule 4: the big answer is reachable from the cards before the final

See the fair-play list at the end of §3. Each card's end meaning rests on a sentence the child has **already read** before the guess it matters for: Mila's choir (case 1) for card 1; »klop-klop« and »ena, dva, tri« (case 2) for cards 2 and 3; »tiho kot mačka« (case 3) for card 2; Rado's keys on his belt (case 4) for card 4. The wrong options are tempting: Rado has the keys, the torch and the night; Ivo can lift the bones and took the tooth.

### 5.5 Rule 5: freshness against the ledger

- **The proof is hidden somewhere other than the object the question names** in cases 2 (cloakroom), 3 (Ivo's desk), 4 (a bag), 5 (behind the curtain), 6 (behind Rex, not on him). Case 1 keeps it on the switch, the gentle exception.

Lan, Zoja, Ivo, Lan, Zoja, Mila. Rado and Mila are both never caught in a small case and both in the guess; Fosil is never a culprit at all (the animal is the decoy this season).

## 6. What is new

### 6.1 New mechanic: 🧭 »Kam kaže sled?« (step `herring`, right before `solve`)

The main session builds it; the puzzle designer specs the hint lines. It reuses most of the UGOVOR screen (two taps, a stamp, its own ladder), with a different meaning and a different look, so the two steps are never confused.
- **What the child sees:** 1–2 **pointing clues** on top, each a sentence card with a fat arrow 👉 to the face it points at (the face is shown next to the card, with a worried look). Below: the other sentences in the pocket.
- **What the child taps:** a pointing clue, then the sentence that **explains it** (»Zakaj kaže sem? Poišči stavek, ki pove, zakaj.«).
- **The catch:** the clue card flips over like a photo, and on its back is the explaining sentence; a blue stamp **»NAPAČNA SLED!«** lands on it, the arrow bends away from the face, and the face sighs with relief 😮‍💨. No shout prompt (UGOVOR keeps the shout; this one is a quiet *aha*).
- **When a clue is true (case 4):** tapping Lan's bag and any sentence says »Ta sled drži. Poišči drugo.« (not counted). This is the guard against »every arrow is wrong«.
- **Wrong kinds and the ladder:** as in UGOVOR (§10.3 E of galeb.md): a gentle note »Ta stavek ne pove, zakaj.« → `h1` → the explaining sentence lights up → the clue and the sentence pulse (assisted).
- **In `solve`:** the flipped clue carries a small »NAPAČNA SLED« tag. Using it against the face it pointed at says »Ta sled vodi drugam. Poglej še enkrat!«; the person still has to be crossed off with an alibi.
- **Pedagogical line for case 5 (no step):** accusing the obvious suspect with an unexplained pointing sentence (`points: 'rado'` on the sentence) gets the level-0 note »Preveč lahko? Poglej še enkrat!«. The debrief counts it (»the obvious suspect blamed: n times«), which is the measure of whether the season taught its lesson.
- **Data idea:** `herring: { clues: ['s2', 's6'], points: { s2: 'ivo', s6: 'lan' }, wrong: ['s2'], explains: { s2: [['s5']] }, h1 }`; for the final, two `wrong` clues, each with its own explanation, both needed before »Vem, kdo je bil!« turns on.
- **Checker:** each `wrong` clue names the face it points at, that face is innocent, and its explanation names nobody as culprit and is not that person's alibi (one sentence, one job). A true clue (`clues` minus `wrong`) is allowed only when it is the proof.

## 8. Risks and how the season avoids them

- **»The obvious one is always innocent« becomes the trick, not the skill:** in case 4 one pointing clue is the real proof; the obvious suspect rotates (Rado, Fosil, Ivo); every innocent is still crossed off only by an alibi in `solve`. The 🧭 step asks *why* a clue points, which is reading, not a guess.
- **»Never Rado« for the big mystery is too easy:** that is the season's lesson, and the guess after case 3 is where we learn whether children resist the obvious one (the guess log shows it). Ivo stays tempting (he took the tooth, he can lift the bones). The final still needs the two explanations, the camera alibi and the pair.
- **Two two-tap steps look alike** (🧭 and ❗): never both in one case; different colours (blue flip vs red stamp), different words (»NAPAČNA SLED!« vs »UGOVOR!«), and the 🧭 shows an arrow to a face, the ❗ a speech bubble.
- **Children skip instructions** (PLAYTEST 2026-11-02): the 🧭 step opens with the instruction card like every step, the owl coaches it once in case 1, and case 1 has no puzzle, so the new step is the only new thing.
- **Missing »ne«:** explanations are positive (»pozabil tu že zjutraj«, »velike kot otroški čevelj«, »previsoko za Fosila«).
- **Clock times are new:** whole hours only, the call and the alibis use the same words, and cases 1–2 keep *zvečer*. Time words are the only raised knob; who speaks stays at Galeb's level.
- **Scary night:** no child is in the museum after eight; the night is only reported in the morning; the shadow, the song and the steps all have a kind person behind them. Mila is a grown-up who lives next door (said in the reveal).
- **A grumpy grown-up made to look bad:** Rado is grumpy, never mean; the child clears him, he asks for their help, and he dances at the end. The letters show his soft side (»Lahko noč, Rex.«).
- **A grown-up sneaking in at night is not a model for children:** Mila is staff, uses the museum's own spare key, and says sorry to Breda and Rado in the letter: »Naslednjič vprašam.«
- **Card 5 points at Zoja (small feet, costumes):** Zoja is never at the museum at night (the children go home at eight in every case) and she is not in the guess or the final; that is fair misdirection, not a trap.
- **Workload:** 🧭 is one new step on the UGOVOR engine (M), witnesses in `ask` (S), the slipper reveal (S), the final's timeline from the calls (S: data only). If 🧭 slips, cases 1–2 can ship with the pointing clue as an ordinary `distractor` and the »Preveč lahko?« note in `solve`, like case 5.
