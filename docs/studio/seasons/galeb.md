# 🦔 Skrivnost hotela Galeb (season 2): pitch

**Status:** pitched (2026-11-01) → **approved (2026-11-01, see §9)** → **written and reviewed (2026-11-01; the game is now the source for all text)** → playtested

> All Slovenian in this file is a draft for the case writers. A native speaker checks it before it goes into the game.

## 1. Title, place, level, rung

- **Title:** 🦔 *Skrivnost hotela Galeb*. Board question: **»Kdo v hotelu piše knjige o Ježku?«**
- **Place:** Hotel Galeb, a small old hotel by the sea in Piran: the beach, the pier, the pool, the roof with its chimney, a long corridor, and room 7 with a balcony. It is summer, but a little spooky at night: typing behind a door, one window lit until dawn.
- **Level:** A (first line on the board: »Stopnja A«). ≤ 8 words a sentence, 4 suspects a case, 7–8 sentences (the final has 9–10).
- **Reading knob raised (only one):** *who speaks*. Last season had narration and short quotes. This season, witnesses say things that **contradict** each other or the scene. Negation stays rare: claims and their contradictions are written in the positive (»Ves dan sem plaval.« / »Kopalke so čisto suhe.«), never as »ni« against »je«.
- **Skill rung 3: the lie, »UGOVOR!«** (confirmed against CURRICULUM.md). Someone says something, and another sentence shows it can't be true. The child already knows that a claim is not proof (Bip 2, Bip 4: »To trdi o sebi«). What is new this season:
  1. **Catching** the lie is an action in its own right, with its own reward.
  2. **A liar is not always the culprit.** People lie to hide something embarrassing or kind.
  3. A witness can lie **about someone else**, to protect them.
  4. Someone who always tells tall tales can still be telling the truth this time.
  5. **The final turns on one lie** that was planted halfway through the season.
- **Old skills mixed in:** crossing off with alibis in every case (rung 1); the corkboard and the guess (rung 2).

## 2. The big mystery and the true answer

- **The big mystery, as a child would say it:** »Nekdo v hotelu na skrivaj piše knjige o detektivu Ježku. In piše o nas!«
- **Background:** the *Detektiv Ježek* books are the books every child in the hotel loves. Nobody knows who writes them, because they are signed with nothing but a hedgehog 🦔. After case 1, pages of a **new** Ježek book start turning up around the hotel. Every page tells, in its own words, the case the child has just solved.
- **The true answer:** **gospa Vera**, the quiet old guest in room 7. She has binoculars and is always knitting a very long blue scarf. »I go to sleep at nine,« she says. It isn't true. She writes all night, because hedgehogs are awake at night; that is why she signs as Ježek. By day she watches from her balcony and turns what she sees into her new book. She hid because she needs quiet to write, and because she wanted to see a real detective at work.
- **The warm ending:**
  - Vera asks the child, »Ali smem napisati knjigo o tebi?«, and the child taps **Da**.
  - The solved screen shows the **cover of the new book**, with the child's ID-card picture and name: *Detektivka Ana in skrivnost hotela Galeb*.
  - The long blue scarf turns out to be a present for the detective, »za zimske primere«. That is the hook for the next season.
- **A wink for the grown-ups:** in 1926 Agatha Christie herself disappeared for eleven days and was found in a hotel under another name. Vera is our gentle nod to that. No real person is named or imitated in the game.

## 3. The red thread

Every card is a **manuscript page** 📄 typed by the hidden author (a change from Bip, where the cards were things found in the scene). The child *reads* each one. No card names a suspect.

- **Planted early, paid off late:**
  - In case 3, Vera says »Ob devetih že spim.« There it is only her own claim, harmless, and she is crossed off by a different alibi. The same case also pins the card about the window lit until dawn. A sharp child may already wonder, but no one can break the claim until the final.
  - The scarf grows longer in every case (a neutral sentence: »Verin šal je že dolg do tal.«).
  - Bepi is awake at night twice (case 3: the pool; case 5: fishing). That makes him tempting, and in the end it makes him the witness who saw the lit window from the sea.
- **The guess (after case 3):** »Kaj misliš, kdo piše knjige o Ježku?«
  - 👧🏽 *Hana, ki ima vse knjige o Ježku* (tempting: the biggest fan, with a notebook, and she snooped at night in case 2)
  - ⚓ *kapitan Bepi, ki pripoveduje zgodbe* (very tempting: he tells stories all day, wears a blue knitted jumper, and was awake at dawn)
  - 👵🏼 *gospa Vera z daljnogledom* (right, but she »sleeps at nine«)
- **New shape (approved):** after case 5 the corkboard asks once more, »Ali še vedno tako misliš?«, and the child may keep or change the guess, the way a detective revises a theory. The final then checks the last choice.
- **Fair-play check (for the producer):**
  - The author is here and writes at night (cards 1 and 2), and someone wrote all night by a window (card 3).
  - The wool fits Vera's knitting or Bepi's jumper (card 4). Only Vera and Bepi are old (card 5).
  - Bepi was out at sea all night.
  - That leaves Vera, who says she sleeps at nine. »UGOVOR!«

## 5. What is new

- **New mechanic: ❗ UGOVOR!** The main session builds it; the puzzle designer specs the hints. **Build spec: §10.3** (its own step `lie`, right before `solve`).
  - On the solve screen (or as its own step), the child taps a claim (a bubble marked 💬 *trdi*), then the sentence that breaks it.
  - A big red **UGOVOR!** stamp slams down (sound, a small shake), and the liar blushes 😳.
  - Then a hidden **truth** sentence arrives and drops into the pocket. Catching a lie *gives* the child something: an alibi (case 3), the nest (case 4), the card (case 2).
  - The hint ladder as usual: »To trdi. Ali je res? Poišči stavek, ki pove drugače.« → the breaking sentence lights up → it pulses.
  - Data idea: `lie: { claim, breaks, truth }`. Checker: the liar may be anyone, not only the culprit. Today `partial` must be said by the culprit, so a new role or field is needed. Season check: the liar is not the culprit in at least 2 cases.
  - **Fallback:** cases 1 and 2 also work with the existing `partial` claim on the solve screen.
- **Smaller builds:**
  - a suspect who is not a person (🌊 *morje*: a character, and a pattern for morje / morja / morju)
  - the **book cover** on the final solved screen (it reuses the newspaper and the ID card), plus Vera's »Ali smem …?« with a Da button
  - corkboard cards drawn as typed pages
  - *optional:* Rozi's reception bell »cin-cin!« instead of the phone ring
  - the second guess after case 5 (approved)
- **New tool for the toolkit:** the red UGOVOR stamp. It is handed over in case 1 (IMPROVEMENTS §6 has the sealed-envelope ceremony, if it is wanted).
- **New feelings:**
  - **catching a fib**: the child can shout UGOVOR! out loud, and the liar blushes and then tells the truth
  - **wonder**: dolphins at dawn (case 3), and a nest with three eggs in a captain's cap (case 4)
  - **being the hero of a book**: someone was writing about *you* all along, and asks your permission
- **New fresh ground from the ledger:**
  - a witness who lies about someone else (case 4)
  - nobody did it (case 5)
  - a recurring rival detective (Hana, friendly; by the final she is cheering for the child)
  - a red thread that is a person to find, not a thing
- **The season's shape changes:**
  - pages instead of found oddities
  - clients rotate
  - the reveal is a familiar face, not a hidden being: no »???«, no eyes in the vent
  - the reward is a book with the child's name
- **Deliberately not repeated:**
  - no space station, no Tim
  - **no food motive** (the motives are: to win, to catch the author, to share dolphins, a nest for eggs, nobody, to write in peace)
  - no »X ima rad Y« decoy (once, »rad« is the real clue instead)
  - no »vso noč« alibis
  - the proof never just says »X did it«: it is a broken claim, a witness, a trace, or a pair
  - two questions that are not »Kdo je …?« (»Kaj je podrlo …?«, »Kdo piše …?«)
  - no footprints as a card: footprints in snow are saved for rung 5

## 7. Risks and how the season avoids them

- **»Lying is fun« or »everyone lies«:**
  - Every lie has a kind or embarrassed reason, and every liar puts it right at once.
  - UGOVOR is said to the *sentence*: the game says »To ni res!«, never »Lažeš!« and never »lažnivec«.
  - In the letters, the people who lied say sorry and something nice happens: Luka teaches Bepi to swim, Hana helps Rozi sweep.
- **»The liar did it« as a pattern:** the liar is innocent in cases 3, 4 and 5. The fair-play tester checks this across the season.
- **Missing the »ne«:** claims and their contradictions are written in the positive (see §1).
- **A typewriter is unknown at 7:** case 2 shows it as a picture with the sound »tik-tak-klink«, and the reveal says »To je pisalni stroj. Z njim se piše.«
- **Night and danger:**
  - No child is alone outside at night. Hana's trap is indoors. Luka rings the bell at dawn, from the hotel terrace.
  - Bepi swims in the pool with armbands, which is funny, not risky.
- **»The sea did it« feels like a cheat:**
  - The question asks »Kaj …?«, which signals that a thing can be the answer, and 🌊 is in the line-up.
  - Two fair traces point to it (seaweed on top, the tide).
- **Vera watching everyone feels creepy:**
  - She watches birds, the sea and the detective, warmly.
  - Her pages are kind about everyone.
  - She *asks permission* before writing the book.
- **Two strong »never caught« suspects** (Bepi, Vera) keep elimination honest. The final still needs the claim, the witness and the pair.
- **The UGOVOR truth makes the solve trivial:** the truth is a lead or an alibi, and the proof comes from a different sentence (except in case 2, the gentle one).
- **Too much reading:** only one knob is raised, and cases 1–2 are 7 sentences. The child's name appears only on the cover (kept on the device, never logged).
- **Workload:**
  - UGOVOR must be built and tested before case 1 is written.
  - If it slips, cases 1–2 can ship on the existing `partial` flow.

**Why this shape**
- **UGOVOR is the season's skill, not a puzzle.** It is a step in every case, like crossing off. The puzzles are counted without it.
- **Case 1 has no puzzle** so the new step has the stage: the owl, the stamp and the shout. Bip 1 had none either.
- **Case 3 has no puzzle** because it already has two investigation steps (ask and scene) and the season's hardest UGOVOR so far.
- **Variety:**
  - No puzzle type comes in two cases in a row (2 cipher · 4 torn · 5 words · 6 cipher + torn).
  - There are **two puzzles the child has never seen** (cipher, torn note) and one known puzzle with a new knob (the geslo).
- **The final combines.** Its two puzzles are the season's two new ones, each one notch harder, and their outputs make the proof pair together. No brand-new mechanic lands in the final.
- **One notch over Bip.**
  - Bip had four puzzles, each new and at its simplest knob.
  - Galeb has five: two new types at their simplest, a known type one notch up, and both new types one notch up again in the final.
  - On top of that, UGOVOR grows over six cases.
- **The outputs vary:** an alibi (2), a breaker (4), an alibi against a decoy (5), the proof pair (6). No puzzle names the culprit as the culprit. The child still links the sentence to a face in `solve`.
- **Puzzle outputs are pocket sentences.** Every puzzle (and the UGOVOR truth) delivers a sentence of the case. It sits in `sentences` with `from: '<step>'` and is not shown in the scene or the questioning (§10.7).
- **The season has 4 puzzle types,** not the ≥ 5 in PUZZLES.md §2. That rule assumes a puzzle in every case; here cases 1 and 3 give their time to UGOVOR.
- **Time per case:** about 6–8 minutes for cases 1–5 and 11–13 for the final. The final is 6 screens long; see question 5 in §10.10.

**The UGOVOR ladder over the season** (one new thing per case):

| # | Claims shown | The lie | The breaker | Liar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | about himself | one sentence in the same scene | the culprit |
| 2 | 1 | about herself | another person's answer | the culprit |
| 3 | 2 (one is the plant: it can't be broken yet) | about himself | a scene sentence against a claim from the questioning | **innocent** |
| 4 | 2 | **about someone else** | a puzzle output from an earlier step | innocent (a protector) |
| 5 | 2 | a tall tale (part true) | one scene sentence | innocent (an exaggerator) |
| 6 | 2 | about herself (repeats case 3) | **two sentences together** | the culprit |

The liar is not the culprit in 3 of 6 cases (the checker wants ≥ 2). The liar is never the same person in two cases in a row (Luka, Hana, Bepi, Hana, Bepi, Vera).

### 10.3 ❗ UGOVOR!: build spec (step `lie`)

**A. Where it sits: its own step, right before `solve`** (recommended), not inside the solve screen.
- **One gesture per screen.**
  - In `solve`, a tap on a sentence and then a face crosses someone off.
  - UGOVOR is a sentence and then a sentence.
  - Two meanings for the same taps on one screen would confuse a 7-year-old. A separate screen keeps each gesture clear.
- **Catching a lie is its own reward** (pitch §1.1). It gets a whole screen: the stamp, the blush, the truth arriving. It doesn't become a side effect of crossing off.
- **The truth is in the pocket before `solve` starts.** So `solve` stays as it is: the truth is just another row (an alibi in case 3, a lead elsewhere), and the alibi and proof rules do not change.
- **Its own hint ladder and its own log events.** The debrief can then show how the child did on the season's skill, apart from the solve.
- **It fits step navigation as built:**
  - `steps: [..., 'lie', 'solve']`, ← goes back one step.
  - The screen is short (30–60 s).
- **The cost: the screen tells the child that something isn't true.** At level A that scaffold is right.
  - The search stays real: from case 3 on there are 2 claims, one of which can't be broken, and the breaker can be on another screen or from a puzzle.
  - A later season can drop the step and move the stamp into `solve` (a knob for level B).
- **The fallback in the pitch still works.** Cases 1–2 can ship with the claim as an ordinary row in `solve`, if the step slips.

**B. What the child sees** (top to bottom)
1. `caseq` (hidden on small screens, as on the other steps) and the qbar »Je vse res? Tapni trditev, nato stavek.« 🔊
2. **Claims** (`lie.claims`, 1–3):
   - Each is a speech bubble with the speaker's face, the name, a small »💬 trdi« label, and the text.
   - They are big tap targets (≥ 56 px tall).
3. **The pocket:** every other sentence the child has found, as rows, in case order (the claims and the not-yet-arrived truth are left out).
   - In the final, a pair breaker can take two selected rows.
   - The rows scroll inside their own box; the footer never moves.
4. **Footer:** ←, »👝 n / total«, and the main button »Vem, kdo je bil!«. It is disabled until the lie is caught (the assist makes sure the child is never stuck).

**C. What the child taps**
- **Claim, then a sentence** (either order is accepted). The claim turns yellow (selected); tapping another claim moves the selection.
- **A sentence with no claim selected:**
  - When there is only one claim, it is selected for the child.
  - Otherwise the note says »Najprej tapni, kaj kdo trdi. 💬« (not counted as wrong).
- **A pair breaker:** the first right sentence stays yellow with »Eden je pravi! Poišči še drugega.«, and the child taps the second.
- **Every tap on a row or a bubble plays its audio,** as in `solve`.

**D. The check**
- A try is a claim plus 1 (or 2) rows. It is right when the claim is `lie.claim` and the rows equal one set in `lie.breaks` (order doesn't matter).
- **Wrong kinds:**
  - `both`: the claim and the rows don't fight. Note: »Oba sta lahko res. Poišči drug stavek.« This is true whichever claim was picked, so it never gives away which claim is the lie.
  - `half`: one row of a pair set. It is not counted; the »Eden je pravi!« note shows.
  - `hmm`: a listed near-miss. It is not counted; its own note shows.
  - `noClaim`: not counted.
- Nothing is checked until the second tap. With 1–3 claims and 6–9 rows, guessing is slower than reading.

**E. The hint ladder** (counted wrong tries, the same rhythm as `solve`)

| Wrong | What happens | Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | toast: the gentle note for the kind (»Oba sta lahko res. Poišči drug stavek.«) | 0 |
| 2 | modal: `lie.h1` if the case has one, else »To trdi. Ali je res? Poišči stavek, ki pove drugače.« (two sentences, each ≤ 8 words) | 1 |
| 3 | modal »Preberi svetel stavek. Se ujema s trditvijo?«: the lie claim is selected for the child, the breaker row(s) light up and the others dim | 2 |
| 4+ | modal »Tapni trditev, nato stavek, ki utripa.«: the claim and the breaker pulse; tapping them does it. Logged as assisted | 3 |

The ladder ends in the answer, so no child is stuck.

**F. Wrong-tap feedback:** a soft wobble of the row and `sfx('nope')`. Never a red X, never »Lažeš«. The words of the game are always about the *sentence* (»To ni res!«), never about the person.

**G. The catch (success)**
1. **First time only:** the shout prompt (J).
2. **The stamp:**
   - A red **UGOVOR!** stamp slams onto the claim bubble (the existing `.stamp.slam`, at the phone size).
   - `sfx('stamp')` plays, then the clip `ui_lieStamp` (»UGOVOR!«, recorded with gusto, so the child shouts along every time).
   - The screen shakes for 300 ms (none with `prefers-reduced-motion`).
3. **The blush:** the liar's face in the bubble gets 😳. The other claim bubbles fade out, and the breaker row(s) flash red once.
4. **The truth:**
   - The liar »types« (the three dots from `ask`) under the stamped bubble, with the toast »Zdaj pove resnico.«
   - The truth bubble arrives and its audio plays, like an answer in `ask`.
5. **Into the pocket:** the truth flies in (the counter goes up) with the toast »Resnica je v žepu! 👝«. The main button turns on.
6. **Going back:** ← from `solve` returns to this screen in its done state; the stamp is not replayed.

**H. Changes in `solve`**
- The broken claim's row carries a small red »UGOVOR!« tag. If the child uses it to cross someone off, the note says »Ta stavek ni res. Na njem je žig.«
- The truth row shows ✨ the first time.
- A truth sentence with `clears` crosses off **its own speaker** (case 3). This is the one exception to »To trdi o sebi«: the selfClaim note must not fire for a `from: 'lie'` sentence.
- **The pedagogical line:**
  - **When:** the child tries to pin the case on an **innocent liar** with the claim, a breaker or the truth.
  - **What:** the level-0 note is »Kdor ne pove resnice, še ni kriv.«
  - **Logging:** `evidence_confirm` gets `liar: true` whenever `suspectId` is the case's liar.

**I. Data format**

```js
// on the case, next to steps; 'lie' is the step just before 'solve'

  claims: ['s2', 's4'],     // 1–3 sentences shown as 💬 claims; each has `who` (the speaker)
  claim: 's2',              // the one that isn't true; the liar is sentence(claim).who
  breaks: [['s6']],         // sets that break it: one sentence, or two that only work together (the final)
  truth: 's9',              // the sentence that arrives after UGOVOR (from: 'lie', who: the liar)
  about: 'gusti',           // optional: the lie is about someone else (case 4); the claim names them
  hmm: [['s4', 's7', 'Hmm … Čigavo okno je to? Bomo videli!']],  // optional near-misses: [claim, sentence, note]

```
- In a scene, the pocket rows should use `sayHTML(s)` (speaker + text) for any sentence with `who`. Today they use the plain text.

**J. The spoken first-time prompt** (once per detective, the first lie step they ever reach, whatever the case number)
- **On entering:** the owl coach (the tutorial's ring and finger, `coachStep()` gets a `lie` branch) shows two steps.
  - The claim bubble: »Nekdo nekaj trdi. Ali je res? Tapni to.«

- **At the catch,** before the stamp:

  - The button waits (no timer). The tap brings the stamp down.
  - This is also the moment the 🟥 UGOVOR stamp joins the toolkit. A sealed-envelope ceremony (IMPROVEMENTS §6) can come later.

**K. Log events** (no names; character ids are fine)

- **Debrief:**
  - Per case: UGOVOR wrong tries and highest hint.
  - Per season: »an innocent liar blamed: n times«, which is the measure of whether the season taught its lesson.
  - Labels go in `EVENT_LABEL`.
  - UGOVOR hints are counted apart from `hints`, so a case's outcome stays comparable with the Bip cases.

**L. Checker rules (`tools/cases.mjs`)**
1. `lie` is in `STEPS`. A case with a `lie` step has `lie`, has exactly one `lie` step, and that step comes right before `solve`. A case with `lie` has no `partial` sentence.
2. `claims`: 1–3 different existing ids. Each has `who` (a suspect of the case), its text is a quote (contains »), and its role is neither `clear` nor `evidence`. `claim` is one of `claims`.
3. `breaks`: 1–3 sets of 1–2 different existing ids. No set holds a claim, the truth, or another `from: 'lie'` sentence.
4. `truth`: exists, has `from: 'lie'` and the liar's `who`, is a quote, and its role is not `evidence` (UGOVOR never hands over the proof). There is exactly one `from: 'lie'` sentence, and it is `lie.truth`.
5. With `about`: the claim text names `about`, and `about` is not the liar.
6. `hmm`: each claim is in `claims`, the sentence exists and is not a breaker, and the note is ≤ 8 words a sentence.
7. Word limits for `h1` and the `hmm` notes. Their clips go in `clipList()` as `<id>_lh1` and `<id>_lm1…`.
8. `from` sentences don't need `obj` / `label` / `ask`, and they don't count in the scene's or the questioning's »n / total« (§10.7).
9. **Per season file:**
   - The liar is not the culprit in **at least 2** of its lie cases.
   - The same person is not the liar in two neighbouring cases.
   - (The fair-play tester checks by reading that no other sentence breaks the claim and no other claim can be broken. The checker can't judge meaning.)

  - The note row (📃, tap to hear).
  - **The key strip:** chips with the symbol on top and the letter below. With `missing`, that chip shows »?«.
  - **The message:** cells grouped by word. Each cell is the symbol (small) over an empty letter slot (big). Digits, punctuation and emoji are shown plain. The first empty cell has a yellow ring; tapping another empty cell moves the ring.
  - **Letter tiles:** only the letters used, plus `extra`, ≥ 48 px each.
  - The footer: ← and the main button, which is disabled until the message is done.
- **Taps:**
  - A tile fills the ringed cell.
  - A right letter fills **every** cell with that symbol (`pop`) and the ring moves on.
  - A wrong letter makes the tile wobble (`nope`).
  - Letters are checked tap by tap; that is fair here, because looking up the key is faster than trying 9–11 tiles.
- **Done:**
  - `tada`, and the message turns into the sentence in ordinary letters.
  - The sentence is **not auto-read**: 🔊 appears and the toast says »Preberi listek!«
  - The sentence goes into the pocket (`gives`) and the main button turns on.
- **Hints** (wrong taps):
  - 1st: the toast »Ta črka ne paše. Poglej ključ.«
  - 2nd: `h1`.
  - 4th: »Poišči svetel znak v ključu.«, with the ringed symbol's chip lit. For the `missing` symbol, `h2` comes instead and gives the letter.
  - 6th+: »Tapni črko, ki utripa.«, with the right tile pulsing.
- **Data:** `cipher: { note, key: { symbol: 'LETTER' }, missing?, extra?: [≤ 2], gives, h1, h2? }`. The message is the `gives` sentence in capitals, so the two can never disagree.
- **Logs:** `puzzle_cipher { correct, n }` · `puzzle_hint` · `puzzle_done { wrong }`. The step is in `screen`, so the final's two puzzles stay apart.
- **Checker:**
  - Every letter of the message has exactly one symbol, and every symbol one letter.
  - The message has ≤ 20 letters and ≤ 9 different letters. `extra` is ≤ 2 tiles, none of them in the message.
  - `missing` is a key symbol that the message uses, `h2` is required with it, and `h2` contains the missing letter.
  - The same symbol means the same letter in every case of the file.
  - The lines are ≤ 8 words; the `gives` sentence has `from: 'cipher'`.
  - The clips are `<id>_cn`, `<id>_ch1` and `<id>_ch2`.
- **SE portrait:**
  - The note (48) and a 5-column key (2 rows, ~104).
  - The message in 2 lines of ~36 px cells (~120).
  - The tiles in 2 rows of 5–6 (~110), and the footer (64).
  - That is about 500 px of 667.
- **Landscape:**
  - The key in one row across the top (9–11 chips × ~60 px).
  - Below it, the message on the left (55 %) and the tiles on the right (45 %, 4 a row), with `caseq` hidden.
  - That is about 330 px of 393.

### 10.5 📜 Raztrgan listek: build spec (step `torn`, built on `order`)
- **What the child sees:**
  - The note row, then the optional `clue` row (🔍 + the pocket sentence, tap to hear).
  - **Slots** in one wrapping line, so the strips read as a sentence (dashed boxes).
  - The deck of strips below, drawn as torn paper (a zigzag `clip-path` on one or both edges).
- **Taps:**
  - A strip in the deck goes to the next empty slot; a strip in a slot goes back.
  - »Preveri ✔« turns on when every slot is full.
  - Right strips turn green and stay; the others go back.
  - An `extra` strip in a slot is simply wrong.
- **Done:**
  - The strips join, with a strip of tape 🩹.
  - The sentence is **not auto-read**; 🔊 appears and the toast says »Listek je cel!«
  - The sentence goes into the pocket and the main button turns on.
- **Hints** (on Preveri, as in `order`):
  - 1st wrong: »Še ni prav. Preberi ga na glas.«
  - 2nd: `h1`; 3rd: `h2`.
  - 4th: »Ta košček pride naslednji. Utripa.«
- **Data:** `torn: { note, parts: [3–4], extra?: [≤ 1], clue?, also?: [[index order]], gives, h1, h2 }`. `also` lists other orders that are also right.
- **Logs:** `puzzle_torn { correct, right }` · `puzzle_hint` · `puzzle_done { wrong }`.
- **Checker:**
  - `parts.join(' ')` is exactly the `gives` text, with 3–4 parts and ≤ 1 extra.
  - With `extra` there must be a `clue` (the content has to decide), and the clue is an existing sentence from an earlier step.
  - The `also` orders are permutations; the lines are ≤ 8 words; the `gives` sentence has `from: 'torn'`.
  - **The Slovenian editor confirms that no other order is grammatical.** Slovenian word order is free, so a torn note can be right in two ways; list them in `also` or change the strips.
- **Layout:**
  - **SE:** note 48, clue 48, slots (2 lines × 56), deck (2 lines × 56) and footer 64, about 400 px.
  - **Landscape:** the slots in one line, the deck in one line below, `caseq` hidden. The slots are not in columns like the timeline, because a sentence reads along a line.

### 10.6 🔤 Iskanka with a geslo (a knob on `words`)
- **Data:** `words.geslo: 's6'` (optional).
- **When the last word is found:**
  - The cells that are not part of any word glow, then fly one by one (~80 ms each, all at once with reduced motion) into a strip.
  - The strip takes the place of the word list, so the height doesn't grow. In the strip the letters are grouped into the words of the sentence.
  - The toast says »Ostale črke povedo skrivnost!«
  - The child reads it. 🔊 comes after, and the ordinary sentence goes into the pocket.
- **Checker:**
  - The letters left over, in row order, equal the `gives` sentence's letters in capitals.
  - The `gives` sentence has `from: 'words'`.
- **Building a grid** is a constraint search: place the words, fill the rest with the geslo in reading order, keep each word exactly once, and run BLOCK. A 30-line script found the case 5 grid at once; keep one like it in `tools/`.
