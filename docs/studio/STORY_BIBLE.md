# Story bible

The world, the people and the voice of Detektivska pisarna. The showrunner owns this file; everyone reads it. When a season adds a person, a place or a running joke, it goes in here.

## The promise to the child

**You are the detective.** Not a helper, not a sidekick: the child gets the phone call, searches the room, crosses off the suspects and names the culprit. Grown-ups in the story ask *them* for help and write *them* a thank-you letter. The game never solves it for them without their tap.

## Tone

- **Cosy mystery**, like Christie's St Mary Mead seen through a 7-year-old's eyes: small places where everyone knows everyone, small mysteries that feel big.
- **Funny.** Every culprit has a funny or kind excuse (»Hotel sem samo en piškot … no, tri.«). Running jokes come back.
- **Warm endings.** The culprit is forgiven, the problem is fixed, and something nice happens (Tim bakes for everyone, Robi gets a proper charger, Bip calls home).
- **A little spooky is fine, scary is not.** Eyes in a vent, a ghost in a museum, a noise at night: always explained, always friendly in the end.
- **Things that happen:** something eaten, borrowed, moved, hidden, broken by accident, a surprise prepared, a prank. **Never:** murder, poison, injury, real theft for greed, a cruel villain, a child in danger.

## Naming rules for new characters

- Short Slovenian names a 7-year-old can read and decline: Tim, Ela, Nik, Pia. Avoid names that look alike in one cast (Tim/Tin).
- Every character needs a name pattern (`re` in `CHARACTERS`) that matches all its case forms, possessives too (Kikin, Elina, Timovo).
- Every character gets **one trait the child can remember** and that can be a clue (Robi's battery, Ela's stars). Traits make good decoys; see [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md) so the same trait isn't a decoy every time.
- Mix boys, girls and grown-ups. **Culprits, suspects and witnesses are people** (the producer, 2026-11-06): animals may live in the world (Meta, Blisk, Gusti), but they are never suspects, culprits or witnesses again, in any new season; a person may wear an animal costume (Bor 🐐 in pokal). Over a season, culprits are spread across the cast.

## A season's shape (the Bip pattern, to vary, not to copy)

Six cases, each with its own small mystery, plus one big mystery that runs underneath (the red thread). Every case pins one card to the corkboard. Halfway, the child guesses the big answer. The final case opens when the others are solved, needs what the child collected, and unmasks the big answer. Letters from clients connect one case to the next. Change at least one part of this shape every season ([CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md)).
